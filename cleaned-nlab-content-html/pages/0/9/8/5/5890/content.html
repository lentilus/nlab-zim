Statement

Throughout, let ℋ\mathcal{H} be a separable infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space.

Theorem

(Kuiper’s theorem)
The topological unitary group U ( ℋ ) \mathrm{U}(\mathcal{H}) in either the

  • norm topology

or the equivalent

  • strong operator topology

  • weak operator topology

  • compact-open topology

is contractible in that there is a left homotopy between the identity id:U(ℋ)→U(ℋ)id \;\colon\; U(\mathcal{H}) \to U(\mathcal{H}) and the constant function const e:U(ℋ)→U(ℋ)const_{\mathrm{e}} \;\colon\; U(\mathcal{H}) \to U(\mathcal{H}).

Similarly, since the general linear group GL(ℋ)GL(\mathcal{H}) of bounded operators is homotopy-equivalent to U ( ℋ ) \mathrm{U}(\mathcal{H}) by the Gram-Schmidt process, it, too, is contractible.

See the commented list of references below.

Related statements

  • the infinite-dimensional sphere is weakly contractible

  • U ( ℋ ) U(\mathcal{H})
