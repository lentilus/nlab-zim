Idea

quantum chromodynamics in 2 spacetime dimensions

First considered by ‘t Hooft 1974 in the large N limit, whence also called the ‘t Hooft model.

Related concepts

  • D=2 Yang-Mills theory

See also the references at D=2 Yang-Mills theory.
