Idea

A manifold is a topological space that is locally isomorphic to a Cartesian space ℝ n\mathbb{R}^n.

A manifold with boundary is a topological space that is locally isomorphic either to an ℝ n\mathbb{R}^n or to a half-space H n={x→∈ℝ n|x n≥0}H^n = \{ \vec x \in \mathbb{R}^n | x^n \geq 0\}.

A manifold with corners is a topological space that is locally isomorphic to an H i n={x→∈ℝ n|x i+1,⋯,x n≥0}H^n_i = \{ \vec x \in \mathbb{R}^n | x^{i+1}, \cdots, x^n \geq 0\} for 0≤i≤n0 \leq i \leq n.

For details see at manifold.

Embedding into the category of diffeological spaces

Proposition

(manifolds with boundaries and corners form full subcategory of diffeological spaces)

The evident functor

SmthMfdWBdrCrn↪AAAADiffeologicalSpaces SmthMfdWBdrCrn \overset{\phantom{AAAA}}{\hookrightarrow} DiffeologicalSpaces

from the category of smooth manifolds with boundaries and corners to that of diffeological spaces is fully faithful, hence is a full subcategory-embedding.

(Iglesias-Zemmour 13, 4.16, Gürer & Iglesias-Zemmour 19)
