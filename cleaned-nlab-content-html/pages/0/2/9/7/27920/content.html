open problems in category theory

This page is meant to list problems in (higher) category theory that have been stated in the literature (here meant in a broad sense including papers, preprints, blog posts, and so on). A problem is considered closed when it is solved by a published paper, after which it should be removed from this page (with a link to the corresponding paper in the comment accompanying the change).

Higher category theory

Open problems in higher category theory:

  • Simpson's conjecture

  • cobordism hypothesis

    an extensive proof sketch was given (and published as such) by Lurie 2009; a proof along a new strategy is claimed by Grady & Pavlov 2021, but not yet published

  • tangle hypothesis

    (closely related to the cobordism hypothesis)
