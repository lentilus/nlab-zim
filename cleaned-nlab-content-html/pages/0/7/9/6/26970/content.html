Idea

If VV is a real orthogonal GG-representation, then the topological little disks operad D |V|D_{|V|} has a G×Σ nG \times \Sigma_n action on its nn-ary space, so it presents an object in the graph model structure on G-operads; under Bonventre‘s genuine operadic nerve, this presents a G-∞-operad called 𝔼 V\mathbb{E}_V.
