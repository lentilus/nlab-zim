Selected writings

On open/closed string duality in interpreting 't Hooft double line notation in the large N limit and in AdS/CFT duality:

  • Rajesh Gopakumar, Cumrun Vafa, On the Gauge Theory/Geometry Correspondence, Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 3 (1999) 1415-1443 (arXiv:hep-th/9811131)

  • Rajesh Gopakumar, Free Field Theory as a String Theory?, Comptes Rendus Physique 5 (2004) 1111-1119 (hep-th/0409233)

Introducing Gopakumar-Vafa invariant and Gopakumar-Vafa duality:

  • Rajesh Gopakumar, Cumrun Vafa, M-Theory and Topological Strings–I (1998), (arXiv:hep-th/9809187, bibcode:1998hep.th….9187G)

  • Rajesh Gopakumar, Cumrun Vafa, M-Theory and Topological Strings–II (1998), (arXiv:hep-th/9812127, bibcode:1998hep.th…12127G)

  • Rajesh Gopakumar, Cumrun Vafa, Topological Gravity as Large N Topological Gauge Theory, Adv. Theor. Math. Phys. 2:413-442, 1998 (arXiv:hep-th/9802016)

Discussion of effective non-commutative geometry exhibited by open M2-branes ending on M5-branes induced by a constant B-field flux density on the M5-worldvolume:

  • Rajesh Gopakumar, Shiraz Minwalla, Nathan Seiberg, Andrew Strominger: OM Theory in Diverse Dimensions, JHEP08 (2000) 008 [doi:10.1088/1126-6708/2000/08/008, arXiv:hep-th/0006062]

On AdS3/CFT2:

  • Lorenz Eberhardt, Matthias Gaberdiel, Rajesh Gopakumar, Deriving the AdS 3/CFT 2AdS_3/CFT_2 Correspondence (arXiv:1911.00378)
