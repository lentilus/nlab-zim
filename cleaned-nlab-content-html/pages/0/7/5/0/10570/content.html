Definition

Given a sequence of inclusions

T⊂B⊂G T \subset B \subset G

where

  • GG is a connected complex reductive algebraic group

  • BB is a Borel subgroup

  • TT is a maximal torus;

this induces

  • the Weyl group W 0=N(T)/TW_0 = N(T)/T;

  • the character lattice 𝔥 ℤ *=Hom(T,ℂ ×)\mathfrak{h}_{\mathbb{Z}}^\ast = Hom(T, \mathbb{C}^\times);

  • the cocharacter lattice 𝔥 ℂ=Hom(ℂ ×,T)\mathfrak{h}_{\mathbb{C}} = Hom(\mathbb{C}^\times, T).

  • a standard parabolic subgroup of GG is a subgroup P JP_J including BB such that G/PG/P is a projective variety;

    parabolic subgroup is one conjugate to the standard parabolic subgroup.

  • the flag variety G/BG/B;

  • the partial flag varieties G/P JG/P_J

A Bruhat decomposition is, if it exists, a coproduct decomposition into a disjoint union of double cosets

G=∐w∈W 0BwB G = \underset{w \in W_0}{\coprod} B w B
G=∐u∈W JBuP j G = \underset{u \in W^J}{\coprod} B u P_j

with

  • W J≔{v∈W 0|vT⊂P J}W_J \coloneqq \{v \in W_0 | v T \subset P_J\}

  • W J≔{cosetrepresentativesuofcosetsinW 0/W J}W^J \coloneqq \{coset\; representatives\; u \; of \; cosets \; in W_0/W_J\}

into Schubert varieties

X w=BwB¯⊂G/B X_w = \overline{B w B} \subset G/B
X u J=BuP J¯⊂G/P J. X_u^J = \overline{B u P_J} \subset G/P_J \,.
