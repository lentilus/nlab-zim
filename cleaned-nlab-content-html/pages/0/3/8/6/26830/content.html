Gorō Shimura

Gorō Shimura (ja. 志村 五郎, 23 Feb. 1930 – 3 May 2019) was a Japanese mathematician, famous for the Taniyama-Shimura conjecture (later known as the modularity theorem after proven by Andrew Wiles).
