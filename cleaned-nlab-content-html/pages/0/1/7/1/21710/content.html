Idea

Where the Chern-Weil homomorphism assigns characteristic forms in de Rham cohomology to principal bundles, the Cheeger-Simons homomorphism refines this to an assignment of secondary characteristic classes in ordinary differential cohomology to principal connections:

GConnections(X) /∼ ⟶cs GCheeger-Simons homomorphism Hom(H •(BG;ℤ),H^ •(X)) ↓ ↓ GBundles(X) /∼ ⟶cw GChern-Weil homomorphism Hom(imv •(𝔤),H dR •(X)) \array{ G Connections(X)_{/\sim} &\overset{ \overset{ \color{blue} \text{Cheeger-Simons homomorphism} }{ cs_G } }{\longrightarrow}& Hom \Big( H^\bullet(B G;\, \mathbb{Z}) \,,\, \widehat H^\bullet(X) \Big) \\ \big\downarrow && \big\downarrow \\ G Bundles(X)_{/\sim} &\underset{ \underset{ \color{blue} \text{Chern-Weil homomorphism} }{cw_G} }{\longrightarrow}& Hom \Big( imv^\bullet(\mathfrak{g}) \,,\, H_{dR}^\bullet(X) \Big) }
