Idea

Reductions of 11-dimensional supergravity on a 4-dimensional space to a 7-dimensional theory.

Properties

U-duality

Maximal 7d supergravity has SL(5,ℤ)SL(5,\mathbb{Z})-U-duality (Kumar-Vafa 96, section 3.4, Rozali 97).

Holography

The AdS7/CFT6-correspondence relates 7-dimensional supergravity on asymptocially 7d anti de Sitter spacetimes to the 6d (2,0)-superconformal QFT.

By reduction from 10d/11d supergravity

The following references discuss construction of full 7d sugra theories or of their solutions by KK-reduction from 11-dimensional supergravity or from 10d type II supergravity.

The construction of 7d supergravity by KK-reduction from 11-dimensional supergravity was first indicated in

  • Peter Freund, M. A. Rubin, Dynamics Of Dimensional Reduction, Phys. Lett. B 97 2 (1980) 233-235 [doi:10.1016/0370-2693(80)90590-0]

via what is now called Freund-Rubin compactification.

That their Ansatz is indeed consistent has been shown in

  • Horatiu Nastase, Diana Vaman, Peter van Nieuwenhuizen, Consistent nonlinear KK reduction of 11d supergravity on AdS 7×S 4AdS_7\times S_4 and self-duality in odd dimensions Phys.Lett. B469 (1999) 96-102 (arXiv:hep-th/9905075)

  • Ali Chamseddine, W. A. Sabra, D=7D=7 SU(2)SU(2) gauged supergravity from D=10D=10 supergravity, Physics Letters B

    476 3-4 (2000) 415-419 [arXiv:hep-th/9911180, doi:10.1016/S0370-2693(00)00129-5]

The SL(5,ℤ)SL(5,\mathbb{Z})-U-duality in 7d is discussed in

  • Alok Kumar, Cumrun Vafa, section 3.4 of U-Manifolds, Phys.Lett. B396 (1997) 85-90 (arXiv:hep-th/9611007)

  • Moshe Rozali, Matrix Theory and U-Duality in Seven Dimensions, Phys.Lett. B400 (1997) 260-264 (arXiv:hep-th/9702136)

Explicit compactifications are discussed in
