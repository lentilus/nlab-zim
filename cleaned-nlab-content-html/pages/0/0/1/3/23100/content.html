Analysis Situs

This page is about the article

  • Henri Poincaré:


    Analysis Situs


    Journal de l’École Polytechnique 2 1 (1895) 1–123


    original: gallica:12148/bpt6k4337198/f7

    English translation by John Stillwell: pdf, pdf

which initiated (cf. Stillwell p. 6) the modern fields of topology (such as in introducing the notion and terminology of homeomorphisms), of algebraic topology (introducing singular homology) and of homotopy theory (introducing the fundamental group).
