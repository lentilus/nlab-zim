Idea

Synthetic algebraic geometry is concerned with synthetic formulations of algebraic geometry, hence with systems of axioms akin to those for synthetic differential geometry but whose natural/intended categorical semantics is specifically in topoi from algebraic geometry (cf. Hakim 1972), such as notably the Zariski topos.

Concretely, in the formulation of Cherubini, Coquand & Hutzler 2024, axioms are added to homotopy type theory which are intended to have HoTT-like categorical semantics in the ( ∞ , 1 ) (\infty,1) -topos version of the Zariski topos. (After a previous formulation, in Wellen/Cherubini 2017-2024, of synthetic differential Cartan geometry in a fragment of differentially cohesive homotopy type theory.)
