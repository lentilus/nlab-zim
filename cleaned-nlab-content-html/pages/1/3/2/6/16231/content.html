Jim Gates

Sylvester James Gates Jr., known as Jim Gates, works on supersymmetry, supergravity and superstring theory.

Selected writings

Formulating supergravity in superspace:

  • Warren Siegel, S. James Gates Jr., Superfield supergravity, Nuclear Physics B 147 1–2 (1979) 77-104 [doi:10.1016/0550-3213(79)90416-4]

  • S. James Gates Jr., Kellogg S. Stelle, Peter C. West Algebraic origins of superspace constraints in supergravity, Nuclear Physics B 169 3–4 (1980) 347-364 [doi:10.1016/0550-3213(80)90037-1]

  • S. James Gates Jr., On-shell and conformal N=4N=4 supergravity in superspace, Nuclear Physics B 213 3 (1983) 409-444 [doi:10.1016/0550-3213(83)90229-8]

On supersymmetry via superspace:

  • Jim Gates Jr, Marcus Grisaru, Martin Roček, Warren Siegel, Superspace, or One thousand and one lessons in supersymmetry, Front. Phys. 58 (1983) 1-548 [arXiv:hep-th/0108200, inspire:195126]

On Green-Schwarz anomaly cancellation in heterotic supergravity:

  • S. James Gates Jr., Hitoshi Nishino: New D=10D = 10, 𝒩=1\mathcal{N} = 1 supergravity coupled to Yang-Mills supermultiplet and anomaly cancellations, Physics Letters B 157 2–3 (1985) 157-163 [doi:10.1016/0370-2693(85)91537-0, spire:16413]

Early consideration of the idea of superstring sigma-models where both the worldsheet as well as the target spacetime are treated as supermanifolds is due (under the name “supersymmetry squared”, cf. the superembedding approach):

  • S. James Gates Jr., Hitoshi Nishino: D=2D = 2 superfield supergravity, local (supersymmetry) 2(supersymmetry)^2 and non-linear σ\sigma-models, Classical and Quantum Gravity 3 3 (1986) 391–399 [doi:10.1088/0264-9381/3/3/013, spire:217518]

  • R. Brooks, F. Muhammad, S. James Gates Jr.: Matter Coupled to D=2D=2 Simple Unidexterous Supergravity, Local (Supersymmetry) 2(Supersymmetry)^2 and Strings, Class. Quant. Grav. 3 5 (1986) 745-751 [doi:10.1088/0264-9381/3/5/005, spire:237390]

On type IIA supergravity formulated in superspace:

  • James L. Carr, S. James Gates Jr., Robert N. Oerter: D=10D = 10, N=2aN = 2a Supergravity in Superspace, Physics Letters B 189 1–2 (1987) 68-74 [doi:10.1016/0370-2693(87)91271-8, spire:235791]
