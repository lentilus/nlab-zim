Serre's criterion of affineness

Idea

Serre’s criterion of affineness characterizes affine morphisms of schemes in terms of exactness properties of the corresponding functors among the categories of quasicoherent sheaves.

If f:X→Yf\colon X\to Y is a quasicompact morphism of algebraic schemes and XX is separated, then ff is affine iff it is cohomologically affine, that is, the direct image functor f *f_* is exact.

EGA II Corollary 5.2.2 Let f:X→Yf:X\to Y be a quasicompact separated morphism of schemes. The following conditions are equivalent:

a) ff is an affine homomorphism.

b) The functor f *f_* is exact on the category of quasicoherent 𝒪 X\mathcal{O}_X-modules.

c) For each quasi-coherent 𝒪 X\mathcal{O}_X-module ℱ\mathcal{F}, we have R 1f *(ℱ)=0\mathbf{R}^1 f_*(\mathcal{F})=0.

c’) For every quasi-coherent sheaf ℐ\mathcal{I} of ideals in 𝒪 X\mathcal{O}_X, we have R 1f *(ℐ)=0\mathbf{R}^1 f_*(\mathcal{I})=0.
