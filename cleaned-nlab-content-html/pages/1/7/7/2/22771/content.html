Idea

The idea of a Heyting scale comes from Peter Freyd.

Definition

In terms of Heyting implication

A Heyting scale or chromatic scale is a scale MM with a Heyting implication operation (−)→(−):M×M→M(-)\rightarrow(-):M \times M \to M such that

  • (⊥→⊥) •=⊥(\bot \rightarrow \bot)^\bullet = \bot

  • for all aa in MM, a∧(a→⊥)=⊥a \wedge (a \rightarrow \bot) = \bot

  • for all aa and bb in MM, ((a∧b)→⊥) •=(a→⊥) •∧(b→⊥) •((a \wedge b) \rightarrow \bot)^\bullet = (a \rightarrow \bot)^\bullet \wedge (b \rightarrow \bot)^\bullet
