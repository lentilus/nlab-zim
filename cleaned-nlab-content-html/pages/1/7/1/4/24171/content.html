2-poset of partial maps

Definition

Given a dagger 2-poset AA, the 2-poset of partial maps Map ⊥(A)Map_\bot(A) is the sub-2-poset whose objects are the objects of AA and whose morphisms are the functional morphisms of AA.

Examples

  • For the dagger 2-poset Rel of sets and relations, the 2-poset of partial maps Map ⊥(Rel)Map_\bot(Rel) is equivalent to the category of sets and partial functions Set ⊥Set_\bot.
