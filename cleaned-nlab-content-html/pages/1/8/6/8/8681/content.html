Lazard's criterion

This page is about characterization of flat modules. For the characterization of the Lazard ring (in formal group laws) see instead at Lazard's theorem.

  • Statement
  • References

Statement

Let RR be a ring, not necessarily commutative.

Proposition

(Lazard’s criterion)

An left RR-module is a flat module precisely if it is a filtered colimit of finitely generated free modules.

This is due to Lazard 1964 and Govorov 1965. See at flat module for more.

Remark

Regarding RR-modules as models of the Lawvere theory of RR-modules, whose representable models are the finitely generated free ones, Lazard’s criterion (Prop. ) splits into two parts:

  1. The formal part is the general fact that a model is a filtered colimit of representable models iff its category of elements is filtered, i.e. iff it is flat as a functor on the theory.

    (This is classical, cf. SGA4 Exp. I; Borceux 1994 §I.6.3. For models of clans, which include models of all algebraic theories via finite-product clans, this is stated as Frey 2026 Lem. 5.4, where it is likewise deduced from Borceux 1994 §I.6.3.)

  2. The genuinely module-theoretic content of Lazard’s criterion is that this categorical flatness coincides with flatness of the module in the sense of homological algebra.
