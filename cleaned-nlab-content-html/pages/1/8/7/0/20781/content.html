prism

This article is on prisms as a geometric shape. A different sense of ‘prism’ is studied in prismatic cohomology.

Overview

The category of prisms is one of the geometric shapes for higher structures. The resulting presheaves of sets are known as multisimplicial sets?. These were used by Laures and McClure in their work on Quinn spectra?.

Definition

The category of nn-fold prisms is the nn-fold product of the category of simplices Δ\Delta.

Presheaves on this category are known as nn-fold multisimplicial sets. Special cases for low nn include sets, simplicial sets and bisimplicial sets.

Restricting to injective maps of simplices, we get nn-fold multisemisimplicial sets.
