Definition

For GG a group and H 1,H 2↪GH_1, H_2 \hookrightarrow G two subgroups, one says that they are conjugate subgroups if there exists an element g∈Gg \in G such that the conjugation action by gg takes H 1H_1 to H 2H_2:

H 1∼ conjH 2≔H 2=Ad g(H 1), H_1 \sim_{conj} H_2 \;\;\; \coloneqq \;\;\; H_2 = Ad_g(H_1) \,,

where Ad g(H 1)=gH 1g −1Ad_g(H_1) = g H_1 g^{-1}.

The equivalence classes under this relation are hence called the conjugacy classes of subgroups of GG. These play a key role in much of group theory and representation theory, for instance as parameters for group characters.
