References

Algebraic topology

Discussion of the algebraic topology (e.g. the topological K-theory) of CY3s:

  • Charles F. Doran, John W. Morgan: Algebraic Topology of Calabi-Yau Threefolds in Toric Varieties, Geom. Topol. 11 (2007) 597-642 [arXiv:math/0605074, arXiv:10.2140/gt.2007.11.597]

In this context, generally on stable splitting of closed 6-manifolds:

  • Ruizhi Huang: Suspension homotopy of 6-manifolds, Algebr. Geom. Topol. 23 (2023) 439-460 [arXiv:2104.04994, doi:10.2140/agt.2023.23.439]

Intermediate Jacobian

Discussion of intermediate Jacobians of Calabi-Yau 3-folds:

  • C. Herbert Clemens, Phillip Griffith, The intermediate Jacobian of the cubic threefold, Annals of Mathematics Second Series, Vol. 95, No. 2 (Mar., 1972), pp. 281-356 (JSTOR)

  • Claire Voisin (pdf)

  • Andreas Höring, Minimal classes on the intermediate Jacobian of a generic cubic threefold, 2008 (pdf)

Discussion of the Artin-Mazur formal groups of CY3s in positive characteristic:

  • Gerard van der Geer, T. Katsura, On the height of Calabi-Yau varieties in positive characteristic (arXiv:math/0302023)

Hall algebra

Discussion of motivic Hall algebras of CY 3-folds is in

  • Maxim Kontsevich, Yan Soibelman, Stability structures, motivic Donaldson-Thomas invariants and cluster transformations (arXiv:0811.2435)
