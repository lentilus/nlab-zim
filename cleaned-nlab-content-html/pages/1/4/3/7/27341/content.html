Idea

A σ\sigma-frame of propositions is a σ\sigma-subframe Σ⊆Ω\Sigma \subseteq \Omega of the frame of truth values Ω\Omega.

However, in dependent type theory, many times we do not have access to the frame of truth values Ω\Omega - which is the type of all propositions Prop\mathrm{Prop} in dependent type theory - because, for example, we want to have canonicity in the dependent type theory, which propositional resizing violates.

Thus, it is beneficial to be able to define a σ\sigma-frame of propositions Σ\Sigma without the need for a type of all propositions Prop\mathrm{Prop}, because it will allow us to define lower, upper, and two-sided Σ\Sigma-Dedekind cuts as well as Σ\Sigma-admissible Archimedean ordered fields, key concepts in predicative constructive real analysis.

Definition

With a family of types

A σ\sigma-frame of propositions consists of

  • a type Σ\Sigma

  • a type family (T(x)) x:Σ(T(x))_{x:\Sigma} satisfying the univalence axiom such that each T(x)T(x) is a subsingleton or h-proposition,

  • an element ⊤:Σ\top:\Sigma such that T(⊤)T(\top) is an singleton or contractible type

  • a function (−)∧(−):Σ×Σ→Σ(-)\wedge(-):\Sigma \times \Sigma \to \Sigma such that for all P:ΣP:\Sigma and Q:ΣQ:\Sigma, T(P∧Q)≃(T(P)×T(Q))T(P \wedge Q) \simeq (T(P) \times T(Q))

  • an element ⊥:Σ\bot:\Sigma such that T(⊥)≃∅T(\bot) \simeq \emptyset

  • a function (−)∨(−):Σ×Σ→Σ(-)\vee(-):\Sigma \times \Sigma \to \Sigma such that for all P:ΣP:\Sigma and Q:ΣQ:\Sigma, T(P∨Q)≃(T(P)∨T(Q))T(P \vee Q) \simeq (T(P) \vee T(Q))

  • a function V:(ℕ→Σ)→ΣV:(\mathbb{N} \to \Sigma) \to \Sigma such that for all sequences f:ℕ→Σf:\mathbb{N} \to \Sigma, T(V(f))≃∃n:ℕ.T(f(n))T(V(f)) \simeq \exists n:\mathbb{N}.T(f(n))

With a type and a function

A σ\sigma-frame of propositions consists of

  • a type Σ\Sigma and a type TT

  • an embedding of types t:T→Σt:T \to \Sigma,

  • satisfying the univalence axiom: for each element P:ΣP:\Sigma and Q:ΣQ:\Sigma, we have an equivalence of types between the identity type P= ΣQP =_\Sigma Q and the type of equivalences between the fiber of tt at PP and the fiber of tt at QQ, where the fiber of tt at PP is given by the type ∑ x:Tt(x)= ΣP\sum_{x:T} t(x) =_\Sigma P. Note that each fiber of tt is a subsingleton because tt is an embedding.

  • an element ⊤:Σ\top:\Sigma such that the fiber of tt at ⊤\top is an singleton or contractible type

  • a function (−)∧(−):Σ×Σ→Σ(-)\wedge(-):\Sigma \times \Sigma \to \Sigma such that for all P:ΣP:\Sigma and Q:ΣQ:\Sigma, the fiber of tt at P∧QP \wedge Q is equivalent to the product of the fiber of tt at PP and the fiber of tt at QQ.

  • an element ⊥:Σ\bot:\Sigma such that fiber of tt at ⊥\bot is the empty type

  • a function (−)∨(−):Σ×Σ→Σ(-)\vee(-):\Sigma \times \Sigma \to \Sigma such that for all P:ΣP:\Sigma and Q:ΣQ:\Sigma, the fiber of tt at P∨QP \vee Q is equivalent to the (-1)-truncated binary sum of the fiber of tt at PP and the fiber of tt at QQ.

  • a function V:(ℕ→Σ)→ΣV:(\mathbb{N} \to \Sigma) \to \Sigma such that for all sequences f:ℕ→Σf:\mathbb{N} \to \Sigma, the fiber of tt at V(f)V(f) is equivalent to the (-1)-truncated dependent sum of the sequence of fibers of tt at f(n)f(n).

 Examples

  • The type of Sierpinski semi-decidable propositions is the initial σ \sigma -frame of propositions.

  • The type of all propositions, if it exists, is the terminal σ\sigma-frame of propositions.

  • Assuming the limited principle of omniscience, the type of booleans is a σ\sigma-frame of propositions.
