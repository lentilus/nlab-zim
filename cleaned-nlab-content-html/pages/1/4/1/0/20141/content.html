Idea

The notion of enriched slice categories is the generalization of the notion of slice categories from plain category theory of locally small categories to enriched category theory of 𝒱\mathcal{V}-enriched categories.

Definition

Definition

Let

  • (V,⊗,I)(V,\otimes,I) be a monoidal category with pullbacks (e.g. a Bénabou cosmos),

  • CC a VV-enriched category,

  • x∈Cx\in C an object.

Then the enriched slice category (or enriched over-category) C/xC/x is the following VV-enriched category:

  • its objects are the morphisms f:y→xf \,\colon\, y\to x in (the underlying ordinary category of) CC, i.e. morphisms I→C(y,x)I \to C(y,x) in VV.

  • its hom-object from f:y→xf \,\colon\, y\to x to g:z→xg \,\colon\, z\to x is the pullback in VV:

Remark

The dual concept is the enriched co-slice category or enriched under-category, the enriched generalization of the notion of co-slice category.

Properties

  • C/xC/x can be described as the comma object of Id:C→CId \,\colon\,C\to C and x:I→Cx \,\colon\, I \to C in the 2-category VCatV Cat, where II denotes the unit enriched category.

  • All hom-objects of C/xC/x are augmented, i.e. equipped with a morphism to the unit object. This seems somewhat curious unless VV is cartesian monoidal (or at least semicartesian) in which case II is the terminal object 11.

  • Any morphism f:x→yf \,\colon\, x\to y in CC induces a VV-enriched functor f !:C/x→C/yf_! \,\colon\, C/x \to C/y. If CC has VV-enriched pullbacks, then f !f_! has a right VV-enriched adjoint f *f^*. If CC is a locally cartesian closed enriched category, then f *f^* has a further right VV-adjoint f *f_*.
