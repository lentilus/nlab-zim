Idea

The quaternionic projective plane ℍP 2\mathbb{H}P^2 is the projective plane over the skew-field of quaternions, hence the quaternionic 2-dimensional quaternionic projective space.

Properties

Cell structure

See at cell structure of projective spaces.

As a quaternion-Kähler manifold / Wolf space

The quaternionic projective plane ℍP 2\mathbb{H}P^2 the first of the list of examples of spaces that are both quaternion-Kähler manifolds as well as symmetric spaces, called Wolf spaces.

AKM-Theorem

In higher dimensional analogy to the Arnold-Kuiper-Massey theorem identifying the quotient of the complex projective plane by its O(1)-action as the 4-sphere, we have:

Proposition

(quaternionic AKM-theorem)

The 7-sphere is the quotient space of the (right-)quaternionic projective plane by the left multiplication action by U(1) ⊂\subset Sp(1):

ℍP 2/U(1)≃S 7 \mathbb{H}P^2 / \mathrm{U}(1) \simeq S^7

(Arnold 99, Atiyah-Witten 01, Sec. 5.5)
