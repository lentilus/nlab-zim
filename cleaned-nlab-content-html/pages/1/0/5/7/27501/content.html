Definition

A group GG is called linear if it admits a monomorphism into a general linear group

G↪GL d(k) G \hookrightarrow GL_d(k)

for some dimension d∈ℕd \in \mathbb{N} and some ground field kk, in other words: it it admits a faithful linear representation of finite-dimensional.
