Idea

By quantum symmetric pairs one means an analogue for quantized enveloping algebras of symmetric pairs in Lie theory (related to symmetric spaces).

While the usual symmetric pairs are pairs of a semisimple Lie algebra 𝔤\mathfrak{g} and an involution θ\theta on it and are classified by Satake diagrams, quantum symmetric pairs are pairs of a Drinfeld-Jimbo quantum group and its right (or left) coideal subalgebra which in the classical limit gives U(𝔤 θ)U(\mathfrak{g}^\theta), where 𝔤 θ\mathfrak{g}^\theta is a θ\theta-invariant subalgebra. However, in the quantum case the right coideal subalgebra is not an invariant subalgebra under some involution in general. There is also another approach to construct qq-analogues of U(𝔤 θ)U(\mathfrak{g}^\theta) depending on solutions of the reflection equation; the two approaches are shown compatible.
