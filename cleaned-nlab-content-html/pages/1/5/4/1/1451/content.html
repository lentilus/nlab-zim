Anders Kock

Anders Kock is a mathematician at Aarhus University, Denmark. He works on category theory/topos theory and particularly on synthetic differential geometry.

Selected writings

On the 2-category of complete categories being monadic over Cat:

  • Anders Kock, Limit monads in categories, PhD thesis (advisor W. Lawvere), University of Chicago (1967) [proquest]

On double dualization monads:

  • Anders Kock: On double dualization monads, Math. Scand. 27 (1970) 151–165 [jstor:24489892, eudml:166154, pdf]

  • Anders Kock, Strong functors and monoidal monads, Arch. Math 23 (1972) 113–120 [doi:10.1007/BF01304852, pdf]
