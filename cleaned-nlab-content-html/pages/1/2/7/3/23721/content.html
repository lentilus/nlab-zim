Definition

Given an Archimedean field FF, an open interval I⊆FI \subseteq F, a pointwise continuous function f:C 0(I,F)f:C^0(I, F) is differentiable if the difference quotient has a limit approaching the diagonal

isDifferentiable(f):=hasLimitApproachingDiagonal(f(x)−f(y)x−y)isDifferentiable(f) := hasLimitApproachingDiagonal\left(\frac{f(x) - f(y)}{x - y}\right)

Let us define the type of differentiable functions D 1(I,F)D^1(I, F) on II as the type of pointwise continuous functions that are differentiable.

D 1(I,F):={f∈C 0(I,F)|isDifferentiable(f)}D^1(I, F) := \{f \in C^0(I, F) \vert isDifferentiable(f)\}

The Newton–Leibniz operator or D operator D˜:D 1(I,F)→(I→F)\tilde{D}: D^1(I, F) \to (I \to F) is a function from the type of differentiable functions D 1(I,F)D^1(I, F) to the type of functions I→FI \to F, and is pointwise defined as

D˜(f)≔lim (x,y)→(x,x)f(x)−f(y)x−y\tilde{D}(f) \coloneqq \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{f(x) - f(y)}{x - y}

for a differentiable function f:D 1(I,F)f:D^1(I, F). For any differentiable function f:D 1(I,F)f:D^1(I, F), the function D˜(f)\tilde{D}(f) is called the derivative of ff.

Properties

These all follow from the algebraic limit theorems of the limit of a binary function approaching a diagonal.

Constant function rule

Let c:I→Fc:I \to F be a constant function. With constant functions the function c:I→Fc:I \to F and the value of the function c:Fc:F are usually written with the same symbol. For all constant functions c:I→Fc:I \to F,

D˜(c)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)c−cx−y\tilde{D}(c) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{c - c}{x - y}
D˜(c)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)0x−y\tilde{D}(c) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{0}{x - y}
D˜(c)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)0\tilde{D}(c) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} 0
D˜(c)=0\tilde{D}(c) = 0

Identity function rule

Where xx\frac{x}{x} is another notation for x⋅x −1x \cdot x^{-1}

D˜(id F)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)x−yx−y\tilde{D}(id_F) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{x - y}{x - y}
D˜(id F)=1\tilde{D}(id_F) = 1

Linearity

For all elements a∈Fa \in F and b∈Fb \in F and functions f:I→Ff:I \to F and g:I→Fg:I \to F,

D˜(a⋅f+b⋅g)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)(a⋅f(x)+b⋅g(x))−(a⋅f(y)+b⋅g(y)x−y\tilde{D}(a \cdot f + b \cdot g) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{(a \cdot f(x) + b \cdot g(x)) - (a \cdot f(y) + b \cdot g(y)}{x - y}
D˜(a⋅f+b⋅g)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)(a⋅f(x)−a⋅f(y))+(b⋅g(x)−b⋅g(y)x−y\tilde{D}(a \cdot f + b \cdot g) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{(a \cdot f(x) - a \cdot f(y)) + (b \cdot g(x) - b \cdot g(y)}{x - y}
D˜(a⋅f+b⋅g)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)(a⋅(f(x)−f(y))+(b⋅(g(x)−g(y))x−y\tilde{D}(a \cdot f + b \cdot g) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{(a \cdot (f(x) - f(y)) + (b \cdot (g(x) - g(y))}{x - y}
D˜(a⋅f+b⋅g)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)a⋅(f(x)−f(y)x−y+b⋅(g(x)−g(y)x−y\tilde{D}(a \cdot f + b \cdot g) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{a \cdot (f(x) - f(y)}{x - y} + \frac{b \cdot (g(x) - g(y)}{x - y}
D˜(a⋅f+b⋅g)=a⋅lim (x,y)→(x,x)(f(x)−f(y)x−y+b⋅lim (x,y)→(x,x)(g(x)−g(y)x−y\tilde{D}(a \cdot f + b \cdot g) = a \cdot \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{(f(x) - f(y)}{x - y} + b \cdot \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{(g(x) - g(y)}{x - y}
D˜(a⋅f+b⋅g)=a⋅D˜(f)+b⋅D˜(g)\tilde{D}(a \cdot f + b \cdot g) = a \cdot \tilde{D}(f) + b \cdot \tilde{D}(g)

Leibniz rule

For all functions f:I→Ff:I \to F and g:I→Fg:I \to F,

D˜(f⋅g)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)(f(x)⋅g(x))−(f(y)⋅g(y)x−y\tilde{D}(f \cdot g) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{(f(x) \cdot g(x)) - (f(y) \cdot g(y)}{x - y}
D˜(f⋅g)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)(f(x)⋅g(x))−(f(x)⋅g(y))+(f(x)⋅g(y))−(f(y)⋅g(y)x−y\tilde{D}(f \cdot g) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{(f(x) \cdot g(x)) - (f(x) \cdot g(y)) + (f(x) \cdot g(y)) - (f(y) \cdot g(y)}{x - y}
D˜(f⋅g)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)(f(x)⋅(g(x)−g(y))+((f(x)−f(y))⋅g(y))x−y\tilde{D}(f \cdot g) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{(f(x) \cdot (g(x) - g(y)) + ((f(x) - f(y)) \cdot g(y))}{x - y}
D˜(f⋅g)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)f(x)⋅(g(x)−g(y)x−y+(f(x)−f(y))⋅g(y)x−y\tilde{D}(f \cdot g) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{f(x) \cdot (g(x) - g(y)}{x - y} + \frac{(f(x) - f(y)) \cdot g(y)}{x - y}
D˜(f⋅g)=f⋅(lim (x,y)→(x,x)g(x)−g(y)x−y)+(lim (x,y)→(x,x)f(x)−f(y)x−y)⋅g\tilde{D}(f \cdot g) = f \cdot \left(\lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{g(x) - g(y)}{x - y}\right) + \left(\lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{f(x) - f(y)}{x - y}\right) \cdot g
D˜(f⋅g)=f⋅D˜(g)+D˜(f)⋅g\tilde{D}(f \cdot g) = f \cdot \tilde{D}(g) + \tilde{D}(f) \cdot g

Power rule

For all functions f:I→Ff:I \to F and natural numbers n∈Nn \in \mathbf{N},

D˜(f n)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)f(x) n−f(y) nx−y\tilde{D}(f^n) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{f(x)^n - f(y)^n}{x - y}

We proceed by induction on the natural numbers. The base case n=0n = 0: For all functions f:I→Ff:I \to F,

D˜(f 0)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)f(x) 0−f(y) 0x−y\tilde{D}(f^0) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{f(x)^0 - f(y)^0}{x - y}
D˜(f 0)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)1−1x−y\tilde{D}(f^0) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{1 - 1}{x - y}
D˜(f 0)=D˜(1)\tilde{D}(f^0) = \tilde{D}(1)

Then the inductive case: For all functions f:I→Ff:I \to F and natural numbers n∈Nn \in \mathbf{N},

D˜(f n+1)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)f(x) n+1−f(y) n+1x−y\tilde{D}(f^{n+1}) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{f(x)^{n+1} - f(y)^{n+1}}{x - y}
D˜(f n+1)=lim (x,y)→(x,x)f(x)⋅f(x) n−f(y)⋅f(y) nx−y\tilde{D}(f^{n+1}) = \lim_{(x, y) \to (x, x)} \frac{f(x) \cdot f(x)^{n} - f(y) \cdot f(y)^{n}}{x - y}
D˜(f n+1)=D˜(f)⋅f n+D˜(f n)⋅f\tilde{D}(f^{n+1}) = \tilde{D}(f) \cdot f^n + \tilde{D}(f^n) \cdot f
D˜(f n+1)=∑ n=0 nD˜(f)⋅f n\tilde{D}(f^{n+1}) = \sum_{n=0}^{n} \tilde{D}(f) \cdot f^n
