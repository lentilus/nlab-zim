Definition

A basis of a free RR-module MM (possibly a vector space, see basis of a vector space) is a linear isomorphism B:M→⊕ i∈IRB\colon M \to \oplus_{i\in I}R to a direct sum of copies of the ring RR, regarded as a module over itself.

We see how this is equivalent to the classical definition of a basis as a linearly independent spanning set:

Lemma

A basis for a free RR-module MM determines a unique generating set for MM of linearly independent elements of MM.
