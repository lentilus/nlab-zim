Idea

The Lie algebra 𝔰𝔩(2,k)\mathfrak{sl}(2,k) (over some ground field kk) is the special linear Lie algebra 𝔰𝔩(n,k)\mathfrak{sl}(n,k) for n=2n = 2.

Over ground field k=ℝk = \mathbb{R} (real numbers) or k=ℂk = \mathbb{C} (complex numbers) this is the Lie algebra corresponding to the Lie group which is the special linear group SL(2,ℝ)SL(2,\mathbb{R}) or SL(2,ℂ)SL(2,\mathbb{C}), respectively.
