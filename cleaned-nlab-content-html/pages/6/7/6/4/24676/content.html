  • Sheng-Fu Chiu, Non-squeezing property of contact balls, doi arXiv:1405.1178

In this paper we solve a contact non-squeezing conjecture proposed by Eliashberg, Kim and Polterovich. Let BR be the open ball of radius RR in ℝ 2n\mathbb{R}^{2n} and let ℝ 2n×S 1\mathbb{R}^{2n}\times S^1 be the prequantization space equipped with the standard contact structure. Following Tamarkin’s idea, we apply microlocal category methods to prove that if RR and rr satisfy 1≤πr 2<πR 21\leq \pi r^2 \lt \pi R^2, then it is impossible to squeeze the contact ball B R×S 1B_R\times S^1 into B r×S 1B_r\times S^1 via compactly supported contact isotopies.
