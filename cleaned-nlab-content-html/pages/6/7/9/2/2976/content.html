Fabien Morel

Fabien Morel is a French algebraic geometer (currently a professor at LMU Munich). His work includes the construction

  • of 𝔸 1\mathbb{A}^1-homotopy theory (two variants, one jointly with Vladimir Voevodsky),

  • of algebraic cobordism with Marc Levine.

and the study of profinite homotopy.
