Idea

The analog of the adjoint functor theorem for indexed categories.

Statement

Theorem

Let 𝒮\mathcal{S} be a cartesian category, let ℂ\mathbb{C} and 𝔻\mathbb{D} be 𝒮\mathcal{S}-indexed categories which are locally small and have all colimits, and suppose further that ℂ\mathbb{C} is well-copowered and has a separating family. Then an indexed functor F:ℂ→𝔻F: \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{D} has an indexed right adjoint precisely iff it is cocontinuous.

This is (Johnstone, theorem B2.4.6).
