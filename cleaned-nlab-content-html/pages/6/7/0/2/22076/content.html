On differential cohomology:

and for KO:

On differential Cohomotopy and Hypothesis H:

  • Daniel Grady, Hisham Sati, Differential cohomotopy versus differential cohomology for M-theory and differential lifts of Postnikov towers, Journal of Geometry and Physics 165 (2021) 104203 [arXiv:2001.07640, doi:10.1016/j.geomphys.2021.104203]

On locality of extended functorial field theory:

On the cobordism hypothesis for extended functorial field theory equipped with geometric structure (such as conformal field theory etc.):

  • Daniel Grady, Dmitri Pavlov: Higher categories of bordisms with geometric structures [arXiv:2605.03453]

  • Daniel Grady, Dmitri Pavlov, The geometric cobordism hypothesis (arXiv:2111.01095)

On the ordinary differential cohomology and differential K-theory of the moduli stack BG conn\mathbf{B}G_{conn} of principal connections for a Lie group GG, in relation to the invariant polynomials for the Lie algebra 𝔤\mathfrak{g}:

Proof that supergravity C-field flux quantization in stable Cohomotopy (stable Hypothesis H) implies the desired divisibility by 6 of the D=11 supergravity action functional:
