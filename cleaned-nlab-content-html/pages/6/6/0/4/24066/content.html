model structure on modules in a monoidal model category

Idea

For 𝒞\mathcal{C} a monoidal model category and A∈Mon(𝒞)A \in Mon(\mathcal{C}) a monoid in 𝒞\mathcal{C}, there is under mild conditions a natural model category structure on its category of modules over AA. (Schwede & Shipley 2000, Thm. 4.1 (3.1 in the preprint)).

Statement

Proposition

Let (𝒞,⊗,𝟙)(\mathcal{C}, \otimes, \mathbb{1}) be a cofibrantly generated monoidal model category and let R∈Mon(𝒞,⊗,𝟙)R \,\in\, Mon(\mathcal{C}, \otimes, \mathbb{1}) be a monoid object whose underlying object is cofibrant in 𝒞\mathcal{C}. Then:

  1. The category RMod(𝒞,⊗,𝟙)R Mod(\mathcal{C}, \otimes, \mathbb{1}) of internal RR-module objects carries a cofibrantly generated model structure whose weak equivalences and fibrations are those whose underlying maps are so in 𝒞\mathcal{C}, hence which is right transferred along the forgetful functor UU:

    RMod(𝒞)⊥ Qu⟶U⟵F𝒞. R Mod(\mathcal{C}) \underoverset {\underset{ U }{\longrightarrow}} {\overset{F}{\longleftarrow}} {\;\;\;\;\;\; \bot_{\mathrlap{Qu}} \;\;\;\;\;\;} \mathcal{C} \,.

  2. If RR is in addition a commutative monoid object then the tensor product of modules makes RMod(𝒞)R Mod(\mathcal{C}) itself into a monoidal model category.

(Schwede & Shipley 2000, Thm. 3.1 with Rem. 3.2)
