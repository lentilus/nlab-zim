derived functor on a derived category

Idea

A special case of the general notion of a derived functor on the homotopy category of a homotopical category is that of a derived functor on the category of chain complexes of an abelian category. This is the original case in which derived functors were considered in homological algebra. This entry discusses special aspects of this special situation.

Derived functors in homological algebra

Here are some peculiarities of the concept of derived functors in homological algebra, mostly due to historical reasons:

  1. Every functor F:C→C′F : C \to C' of abelian categories canonically induces a functor K(F):K(C)→K(C′)K(F) : K(C) \to K(C') of categories of chain complexes. The (right, say) derived functor RFR F of FF is the derived functor of K(F)K(F) and usually only functors of chain complexes of the form K(F)K(F) are considered.

  2. Similarly the output of the derived functor is usually taken to be in C′C' by postcomposing with the homology functor. One writes R kF:=H k∘RFR^k F := H^k \circ R F. The derived functor RFR F in its totality as a functor with values in K(C)K(C) is then sometimes denoted R •FR^\bullet F.
