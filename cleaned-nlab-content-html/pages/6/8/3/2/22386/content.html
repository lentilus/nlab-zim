Statement

Proposition

(Cartan's closed subgroup theorem)

If H⊂GH \subset G is a closed subgroup of a (finite dimensional) Lie group, then HH is a sub-Lie group, hence a smooth submanifold such that its group operations are smooth functions with respect to the submanifold smooth structure.

(due to Cartan 52, see Lee 12, Thm. 20.12)

Consequences

  • continuous homomorphisms of Lie groups are smooth

  • coset space coprojection admitting local sections
