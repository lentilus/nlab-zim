Mostow rigidity theorem

Statement

If a 3-manifold Σ\Sigma (or more generally a smooth manifold of dimension ≥3\geq 3) admits the structure of a hyperbolic manifold of finite volume, then this structure is unique up to isometry, and in fact it is uniquely determined already by the fundamental group π 1(Σ)\pi_1(\Sigma).

(Mostow 68).
