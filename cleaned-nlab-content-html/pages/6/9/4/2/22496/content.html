Idea

For GG a group, there is a pointed connected groupoid (G⇉*)(G \rightrightarrows \ast) with

  • a single object,

  • morphisms the elements of GG,

  • composition the group operation in GG.

This may be understood as the “delooping” of GG in the ambient (infinity,1)-topos, and is, as such, naturally denoted BG\mathbf{B}G.
