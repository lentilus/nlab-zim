Idea

The notion of cartesian closed 2-category is the analog in 2-category theory (hence the categorification) of the notion of cartesian closed category in category theory:

A cartesian closed 2-category is a 2-category, ℬ\mathcal{B}, with finite products and a cartesian closed structure: For any AA, BB in ℬ\mathcal{B}, there is an exponential object B AB^A, an evaluation 1-morphism, eval A,B:B A×A→Beval_{A, B}: B^A \times A \to B, and for every XX an adjoint equivalence between ℬ(X,B A)\mathcal{B}(X, B^A) and ℬ(X×A,B)\mathcal{B}(X \times A, B).

The concept was introduced in (Makkai 96). There is no connection to the concept of cartesian bicategory.

Examples

The following 2-categories are cartesian closed:

  • the 2-categories Cat(𝒞\mathcal{C}) of internal categories or Grpd(𝒞)(\mathcal{C}) of internal groupoids internal to a cartesian closed category 𝒞\mathcal{C} (e.g. Niefield & Pronk 2019);

  • the 2-category of generalized species (FGHW);

  • the 2-category of cartesian distributors;

  • the 2-category of coloured operads and bimodules.
