Idea

An 11-manifold is a manifold of dimension 11. (Generally a topological manifold, but it can be specified to a PL manifold or a smooth manifold.) 11-manifolds are of particular interest as underlying spacetime in M-theory and its low energy limit in D=11 supergravity.

Examples

  • 11-sphere
  • real projective spaceℝP 11\mathbb{R}P^11

Properties

Proposition

For a smooth 11-manifold MM, its last three Stiefel-Whitney classes vanish:

w 9(M)=w 10(M)=w 11(M)=0. w_9(M) =w_10(M) =w_11(M) =0.

More generally, for every 4n+34n+3-dimensional smooth manifold, one has w 4n+1=w 4n+2(M)=w 4n+3(M)=0w_{4n+1}=w_{4n+2}(M)=w_{4n+3}(M)=0. Hence this property also holds for 3-manifolds, 7-manifolds and 15-manifolds.
