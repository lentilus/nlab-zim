Idea

Where a vertex operator algebra encodes one chiral half of a 2d CFT, a full field algebra combines two vertex operator algebras to produce a genuine 2d CFT defined on the complex plane (i.e. on genus 0, notice that to define it on all genera one needs still more information, see at FRS-theorem).
