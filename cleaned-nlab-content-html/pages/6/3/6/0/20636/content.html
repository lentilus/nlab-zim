Christina Vasilakopoulou

Finished PhD in 2014 at Cambridge with Martin Hyland. She works on enriched categories, categorical algebra, and applied category theory. Christina is currently an Assistant Professor at the School of Applied Mathematical and Physical Sciences, National Technical University of Athens, Greece.
