Statement

If for a triangle the radius of its circumsphere is RR, the radius of its insphere is rr and the distances of the centers of both spheres is dd, then these are related by:

d 2=R(R+2r). d^2 =R(R+2r).

Euler's theorem in geometry can be generalized to higher dimensions, but then becomes an inequality:

  • n=2n=2: Euler's theorem in geometry
  • n=3n=3: Grace-Danielsson inequality
  • n≥4n\geq 4: Egan-Drozdov inequality

History

The Euler’s theorem in geometry was independently proved by William Chapple in Chapple 46 and Leonhard Euler in 1765.
