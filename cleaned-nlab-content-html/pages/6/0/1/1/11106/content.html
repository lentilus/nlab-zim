Idea

Linear implication D⊸ED \multimap E is the most common version of implication/function type in linear logic/linear type theory.

The categorical semantics of linear implication is as the internal hom in the non-cartesian closed monoidal category of types whose tensor product ⊗\otimes corresponds to multiplicative conjunction.

Closely related is the multiplicative implication of bunched implication logic, though they behave somewhat differently.
