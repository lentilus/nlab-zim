Definition

Let CC be a coherent category. Its category of filters, denoted by ΛC\Lambda C is the category whose objects are pairs (A,F)(A,F), where A∈CA\in C and F⊆sub C(A)F\subseteq \sub_C(A) is a filter. A morphism (A,F)→(B,G)(A,F)\to(B,G) in ΛC\Lambda C is a germ [α][\alpha] of local continuous maps, where a local continuous map α:(A,F)→(B,G)\alpha:(A,F)\to(B,G) is a morphism α:U→B\alpha:U\to B in CC, where U∈FU\in F, such that for all V∈GV\in G, we have α *(V)∈F\alpha^*(V)\in F. Two such maps α 1:U 1→B\alpha_1:U_1\to B and α 2:U 2→B\alpha_2:U_2\to B are equivalent iff there exist U∈FU\in F with U≤U 1∧U 2U\leq U_1\wedge U_2 and α 1| U=α 2| U\alpha_1 |_U = \alpha_2|_U.

The topos of types of CC, denoted by T(C)\mathbf{T}(C), is sheaf topos sh(τC,J p)sh(\tau C, J_p), where τC\tau C is the full subcategory of ΛC\Lambda C consisting of all pairs (A,ρ)(A,\rho) where ρ⊆sub C(A)\rho\subseteq\sub_C(A) is a prime filter, and J pJ_p is the topology induced on τC\tau C by the coherent topology on ΛC\Lambda C.

This definition first appeared in Makkai.

In particular, we can describe the topology J pJ_p as the topology generated by the singleton covers, i.e. a sieve SS on (A,ρ)(A,\rho) is covering in (\tau C, J_p) if and only if it contains an arrow [α]:(B,σ)→(A,ρ)[\alpha]:(B,\sigma)\to(A,\rho) such that ∃ [α]σ=ρ\exists_{[\alpha]}\sigma = \rho, or more explicitly $ρ={V∈sub C(A):α *(V)∈σ}.\rho = \{V\in \sub_\C(A) : \alpha^*(V) \in \sigma\}. For more details see (Coumans, Section 5)

Note: The word “types” here has nothing to do with [[type theory]]. Instead, refers to the notion of [[type (in model theory)|type in model theory]].

Properties

In terms of the coherent hyperdoctrine

Let CC be a [[coherent category]]. The [[canonical extension]] 𝒮 C δ\mathcal{S}_C^\delta of its [[coherent hyperdoctrine]] 𝒮 C\mathcal{S}_C is naturally an [[internal locale]] in the [[presheaf topos]] [C op,Set][C^{op}, Set] (as well as in the [[sheaf topos]] Sh coh(C)Sh_{coh}(C) for the [[coherent topology]] on CC).

The topos of types is equivalent to the topos of [[internal sheaves]] over this [[internal locale]].

T(C)≃Sh(𝒮 C δ). \mathbf{T}(C) \simeq Sh(\mathcal{S}_C^\delta) \,.

This is due to (Coumans, theorem 25).

In particular this implies that there is a [[localic geometric morphism]]

T(C)→Sh coh(C). \mathbf{T}(C) \to Sh_{coh}(C) \,.

Let Mod(C)≃Func coh(C,Set)Mod(C) \simeq Func_{coh}(C,Set) be the category of [[models]] of the [[coherent theory]] given by CC in [[Set]].

There is a [[full subcategory]] 𝒦↪Mod(C)\mathcal{K} \hookrightarrow Mod(C) such that the [[Yoneda extension]] of the [[evaluation map]] ev:C→Func(𝒦,Set)ev : C \to Func(\mathcal{K}, Set) is the [[inverse image]] of a [[geometric morphism]]

ϕ ev:Set 𝒦→Sh coh(C). \phi_{ev} : Set^{\mathcal{K}} \to Sh_{coh}(C) \,.

The [[(hyperconnected, localic) factorization system|hyperconnected / localic factorization]] of this morphism is through the topos of types of CC:

ϕ ev:Set 𝒦→hyperconn.T(C)→localicSh coh(C). \phi_{ev} : Set^{\mathcal{K}} \stackrel{hyperconn.}{\to} \mathbf{T}(C) \stackrel{localic}{\to} Sh_{coh}(C) \,.

This is (Coumans, theorem 39).
