Selected writings

On holographic superconductors via AdS4/CFT3 duality (i.e. with the bulk in 11d supergravity aka M-theory):

  • Steven S. Gubser, Silviu S. Pufu, Fabio D. Rocha: Quantum critical superconductors in string theory and M-theory, Phys. Lett. B 683 (2010) 201-204 [arXiv:0908.0011, doi:10.1016/j.physletb.2009.12.017]

Discussion of small N effects in M-theory using the conformal bootstrap, specifically for the D=3 SCFT (BLG-model, ABJM model) on the M2-brane via AdS4/CFT3:

  • Nathan B. Agmon, Shai Chester, Silviu S. Pufu: Solving M-theory with the Conformal Bootstrap, J. High Energ. Phys. 2018 159 (2018). [arXiv:1711.07343, doi:10.1007/JHEP06(2018)159]

  • Nathan B. Agmon, Shai Chester, Silviu S. Pufu: The M-theory Archipelago, J. High Energ. Phys. 2020 10 (2020) [doi:10.1007/JHEP02(2020)010, arXiv:1907.13222]

  • Damon J. Binder, Shai Chester, Max Jerdee, Silviu S. Pufu: The 3d 𝒩=6\mathcal{N}=6 Bootstrap: From Higher Spins to Strings to Membranes, J. High Energ. Phys. 2021 83 (2021) [arXiv:2011.05728, doi:10.1007/JHEP05(2021)083]

  • Shai M. Chester, Silviu S. Pufu, Yifan Wang, Xi Yin: Bootstrapping M-theory Orbifolds [arXiv:2312.13112]

  • Shai M. Chester, Ross Dempsey, Silviu S. Pufu: Higher-derivative corrections in M-theory from precision numerical bootstrap [arXiv:2412.14094]

Discussion of (abelian) Chern-Simons theory on M5-branes:

  • Márk Mezei, Silviu S. Pufu, Yifan Wang: Chern-Simons theory from M5-branes and calibrated M2-branes, J. High Energ. Phys. 2019 165 (2019) [doi:10.1007/JHEP08(2019)165, arXiv:1812.07572]
