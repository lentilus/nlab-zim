Idea

A notion intermediate of those of bisimplicial set and bicubical set

Definition

A cubical-simplicial set is a functor

F:□ op×Δ op→Set F : \Box^{op} \times \Delta^{op} \to \mathrm{Set}

where □\Box is a category of cubes and Δ\Delta is the simplex category.

This is the same as a cubical object in the category of simplicial sets.

Examples

  • triangulated type theory has categorical semantics in cubical-simplicial sets where the category of cubes used is the category of finite flat distributive lattices.
