Idea

The term “system L” may refer to (at least) two different concepts in formal logic:

  • there is a syntax for sequent calculus introduced in (Curien-Herbelin 00) under the name λμ¯μ\lambda \bar \mu \mu-calculus, whose name then changed in time to “system L” in reference to the “L” in “LK”, “LJ”, from Gerhard Gentzen‘s original terminology of sequent calculi;

  • there is a natural deduction system of this name, see System L.
