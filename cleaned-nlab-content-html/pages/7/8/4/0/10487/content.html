Xiao-Gang Wen

Xiao-Gang Wen is a professor in theoretical physics at MIT. His expertise is in condensed matter theory of strongly correlated electronic systems and topological phases of matter. He introduced and studied the concept of topological order and its symmetry proteced versions. Topological orders also classify finite generalized symmetries in one lower dimensions.
