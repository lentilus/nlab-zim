Idea

A chromatic spectral sequence is a spectral sequence of a filtered stable homotopy type for the case of a filtering given by a chromatic tower. (Wilson 13)

This is a type of spectral sequence useful for computing the E 1E^1-term in an Adams-Novikov spectral sequence. The traditional formulation is due to Doug Ravenel.
