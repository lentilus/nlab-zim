Idea

A Fell Bundle is a family of Banach spaces that varies continuously over the morphism space of a topological groupoid, where nontrivial morphisms in the groupoid induce a bilinear composition across the fibers. They are a generalization of both continuous fields of C*-algebras over topological spaces and of C *C^*-algebraic bundles (fiber bundles of C *C^*-algebras over topological groups).

Definition

Definition

A Fell Bundle over a groupoid 𝒢\mathcal{G} is a “continuous” functor E:𝒢→ℭ𝔬𝔯𝔯E \colon \mathcal{G} \to\mathfrak{Corr}, where ℭ𝔬𝔯𝔯\mathfrak{Corr} is the 22-category of C *C^*-algebras and C *C^*- correspondences.

Banach algebra bundles

Mulvey considers the special case where the groupoid is a topological space and proves that such Banach space bundles are equivalent to sheaves of Banach spaces, and that both are equivalent to Banach spaces internal to the sheaf topos.
