Definition

Definition

(weak orthogonal class/Quillen negation)
Given a class M⊂Mor(𝒞)M \;\subset\; Mor(\mathcal{C}) of morphisms in a category 𝒞\mathcal{C}, its

  • left weak orthogonal class or left Quillen negation

    M ⧄⊂Mor(𝒞)\multiscripts{^&solb;}{M}{} \;\subset\; Mor(\mathcal{C})

    (also denoted MProjM Proj or similar, for MM-projective morphisms)

or

  • right weak orthogonal class or right Quillen negation

    M ⧄⊂Mor(𝒞)\multiscripts{}{M}{^&solb;} \;\subset\; Mor(\mathcal{C})

    (also denoted MInjM Inj or similar, for MM-injective morphisms)

is the class of morphisms in 𝒞\mathcal{C} which have the left, respectively right, lifting property with respect to each morphism in the class MM:

M ⧄≔{p∈Mor(𝒞)|∀i∈Mi⧄p}, ⧄M≔{i∈Mor(𝒞)|∀p∈Mi⧄p}. M^{&solb;} \;\coloneqq\; \Big\{ p \,\in\, Mor(\mathcal{C}) \;\big\vert\; \underset{i \in M}{\forall} \; i \,&solb;\, p \Big\}, \;\;\;\;\; {}^{&solb;}M \;\coloneqq\; \Big\{ i \,\in\, Mor(\mathcal{C}) \;\big\vert\; \underset{p \in M}{\forall} \; i \,&solb;\, p \Big\} \,.

Properties

Proposition

(closure properties)
A right weak-orthogonal class C ⧄C^{&solb;} is always closed under retracts, pullbacks, (small) products (whenever they exist in the category) and composition of morphisms, and contains all isomorphisms of C.

Meanwhile, a left weak-orthogonal class ⧄M{}^{&solb;}M is closed under retracts, pushout, (small) coproduct and transfinite composition (filtered colimits) of morphisms (whenever they exist in the category), and also contains all isomorphisms.

This proof is straightforward, see for instance here at injective or projective morphism.

Remark

It is clear that

C⊂( ⧄M) ⧄,C⊂(M ⧄) ⧄. C \;\subset\; \big({}^{&solb;}M\big)^{&solb;} \,, \;\;\; C \;\subset\; \multiscripts{^&solb;}{\big(M^{&solb;}\big)}{} \,.

Examples

Example

In the category Set of sets, the right Quillen negation of the “simplest non-surjection” ∅→*\varnothing \to \ast (the unique map from the empty set to the singleton set) is the class of surjections:

{∅→*} ⧄={f|fis surjective}. \big\{ \varnothing \to \ast \big\}^{&solb;} \;=\; \big\{ f \;\big\vert\; f \; \text{is surjective} \big\} \,.

The left and right Quillen negations of {x 1,x 2}→*\{x_1,x_2\} \to \ast (the “simplest non-injection”) are both precisely the class of injections,

⧄{{x 1,x 2}⟶*}={{x 1,x 2}⟶*} ⧄={f|f is injective }. {}^{&solb;} \big\{ \{x_1,x_2\} \longrightarrow \ast \big\} \;=\; \big\{ \{x_1,x_2\} \longrightarrow \ast \big\}^{&solb;} \;=\; \big\{ f \;\big\vert\; f \; \text{ is injective } \big\} \,.
