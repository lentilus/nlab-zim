Statement

The infinite product formula for the sine function, due to Euler, is

sinx=x∏ n=1 ∞(1−x 2π 2n 2).\sin x = x \prod_{n=1}^\infty (1 - \frac{x^2}{\pi^2 n^2}).

Taking logarithms and then derivatives, this implies the formula

πcotπx=1x+∑ n=1 ∞(1x+n+1x−n).\pi \cot \pi x = \frac1{x} + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \left(\frac1{x+n} + \frac1{x-n} \right).

Conversely, assuming this cotangent identity, then integrating and exponentiating, we deduce that the product formula is correct up to a constant factor CC, and we further deduce that C=1C = 1 because limx→0sinxx=1\underset{x \to 0}{\lim}\; \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1. In this sense, we may say that the product formula is equivalent to this expansion formula for the cotangent.
