category over a category

In SGA I.6 Grothendieck and Pierre Gabriel presented the formalism of fibered categories and descent; the exposition and the terminology emphasises a version of the relative point of view for categories. Thus they start with preliminaries over the slice 2-category Cat/CCat/C of functors with fixed target category CC. Objects in Cat/CCat/C are thus called categories over CC and the morphisms are called functors over CC. Standard passages between different slice categories are via the base change and the cobase change 2-functors.

An important sub-2-category of Cat/CCat/C is the 2-category of fibered categories over CC; this is not a full sub-2-category: one restricts only to cartesian functors among fibered categories. The 2-categories Cat/CCat/C are fibers in a codomain 2-fibration Cat 2Cat^2 over CatCat.
