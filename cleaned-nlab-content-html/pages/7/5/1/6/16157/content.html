dg-Yoneda embedding

Idea

The dg-Yoneda embedding is the analogue of the Yoneda embedding in the setting of dg-categories.

Definition

Let dg−mod Tdg-mod_T denote the dg-category of dg-modules over TT. The enriched Yoneda embedding is a fully faithful functor of dg-categories

h:T↪dg−mod T. h : T \hookrightarrow dg-mod_T.

This induces the dg-Yoneda embedding

h:T↪D(T) h : T \hookrightarrow D(T)

into the derived dg-category of TT, which is a full sub-dg-category of dg−mod Tdg-mod_T.
