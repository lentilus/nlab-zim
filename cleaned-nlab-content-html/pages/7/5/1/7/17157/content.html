Statement

For SS any spectrum and HAH A an Eilenberg-MacLane spectrum, then the smash product S∧HAS\wedge H A (the AA-ordinary homology spectrum) is non-canonically equivalent to a product of EM-spectra (hence a wedge sum of EM-spectra in the finite case).

(Adams 74, part II, lemma 6.1)
