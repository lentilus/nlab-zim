Idea

A procedure for producing (Lagrangians for) topological quantum field theories from non-topological but supersymmetric QFTs.

Examples

  • Topological twists of the type II superstring are the A-model, B-model and half-twisted model versions of the topological string.

  • For the topological twist of D=4 super Yang-Mills theory see at topologically twisted D=4 super Yang-Mills theory.

  • A topological twist of D=3 N=4 super Yang-Mills theory is Rozansky-Witten theory.

  • topologically twisted supergravity

Related concepts

  • cohomological field theory, TCFT

References

The influential idea goes back to

Mathematical formalization and classification is discussed in

  • Kevin Costello, Notes on supersymmetric and holomorphic field theories in dimension 2 and 4, talk at Geometric and Algebraic Structures in Mathematics, Stony Brook (2011) [doi, arXiv:1111.4234]

  • Chris Elliott, Pavel Safronov, Topological twists of supersymmetric observables, Commun. Math. Phys. 371 2 (2019) 727-786 [doi, arXiv:1805.10806]

  • Richard Eager, Ingmar Saberi, Johannes Walcher, Nilpotence varieties, Annales Henri Poincare 22 4 (2021) 1319-1376 [doi, arXiv:1807.03766]

For more see the references at topologically twisted D=4 super Yang-Mills theory.

On conformal twists of super-conformal field theories:

  • Chris Elliott, Owen Gwilliam, Matteo Lotito. Twists of superconformal algebras (2024). [arXiv:2403.19753]

On a general approach to topological twisting D = 4 D=4 N = 2 N=2 SYM theories based on a notion of transfer of structure group, and proposals for twisting non-Lagrangian theories (such as theories of Class S):

  • Gregory W. Moore, Vivek Saxena, Ranveer Kumar Singh: Topological Twisting of 4d N=2N=2 Supersymmetric Field Theories [arXiv:2411.14396]
