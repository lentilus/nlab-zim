Cartan-Dirac structure on a Lie group

Contents

  • Idea
  • References

Idea

For GG a Lie group and ⟨−,−⟩\langle -,-\rangle a non-degenerate binary invariant polynomial on its Lie algebra 𝔤\mathfrak{g}, there is a canonical Dirac structure on GG, a subbundle of the generalized tangent bundle TG⊕T *GT G \oplus T^* G which is maximal isotropic with respect to the canonical pairing and preserved by the Courant bracket twisted by the canonical differential 3-form ⟨−,[−,−]⟩\langle -,[-,-]\rangle on GG.

The leaves of this structure are the conjugacy classes of GG. There is a differential 2-forms θ g\theta_g on each conjugacy class ι:𝒞↪G\iota \colon \mathcal{C} \hookrightarrow G, such that dθ g=ι *⟨−,[−,−]⟩d \theta_g = \iota^* \langle -,[-,-]\rangle.

This Dirac structure was first observed in (Ševera-Weinstein). The name “Cartan-Dirac structure” was introduced in (BCWZ).
