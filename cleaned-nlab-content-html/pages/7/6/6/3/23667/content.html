Idea

The essentially algebraic structure of partial endofunctions on a set SS, and specific cases for when SS is an abelian group, commutative ring, and field respectively.

Definition

In a field

If SS is a Heyting field, then for every subset A∈Ob(Part(S))A \in Ob(Part(S)), Hom(A,ℑ(S))Hom(A, \Im(S)) is a SS-commutative algebra, with global operators corresponding to composition, addition, negation, and multiplication of partial functions. Let
