Idea

Forming the kernel of an adic completion map is sometimes called forming the adic residual, for instance the pp-residual for pp-adic completion.

Properties

Explicit characterization

For AA a commutative ring and 𝔞⊂A\mathfrak{a} \subset A an ideal, and NN an AA-module, then the 𝔞\mathfrak{a}-adic residual of NN (hence the kernel of the map to the completion of a module N⟶N 𝔞 ∧N \longrightarrow N^\wedge_{\mathfrak{a}}) is equivalently the submodule of elements annihilated by 1+𝔞1 + \mathfrak{a}.
