Monodromy — Cubical Agda

The construction of monodromy in homotopy type theory can be expressed through transport along paths in dependent type families.

Formalization: Transport equivalences, loop-space monodromy, pointed transport, and induced equivalences on higher homotopy groups.

Verification: Agda 2.8.0, Cubical Library v0.9 — type-checked.

Scope limitation: The complete fundamental-group action on fixed-basepoint higher homotopy groups has not yet been formalized.
