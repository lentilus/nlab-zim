Idea

Given a system of finitely many word equations

F α(a 1,…,a n,x 1,…,x m)=1,whereα∈{1,…,K} F_\alpha(a_1,\ldots,a_n,x_1,\ldots,x_m) = 1 \,, \;\;\; \text{where}\; \alpha \in \{1,\ldots, K\}

with coefficients a 1,…,a na_1,\ldots, a_n in a group GG, a solution in GG for unknowns x 1,…,x mx_1,\ldots,x_m supplies the evaluation “functional” in Hom(G F,G)Hom(G_F,G), where G FG_F is the direct product of n+mn+m copies of GG modulo the normal subgroup determined by F αF_\alpha for fixed constants a ia_i.

Makanin 1982 found an algorithm which for such a finite system of equations over a free group on ss letters produces all solutions when at least one exists. In his thesis, Razborov 1984 developed the theory further.

Later, similar schemes, now called Makanin-Razborov diagrams, for finding Hom(H,G)Hom(H,G) in the larger generality where HH is some finitely generated group, were discussed by various authors, including Sela, Reinfeldt, Weidmann, Bestvina, and Feighn.
