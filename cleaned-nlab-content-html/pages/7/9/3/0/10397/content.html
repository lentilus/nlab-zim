  • David Berenstein, Juan Maldacena, Horatiu Nastase, Section 2 of: Strings in flat space and pp waves from 𝒩=4\mathcal{N} = 4 Super Yang Mills, JHEP 0204 (2002) 013 (arXiv:hep-th/0202021)

On flat space holography:
