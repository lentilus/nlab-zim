Idea

For inner derivation Lie 2-algebra inn(𝔤)inn(\mathfrak{g}) of a Lie algebra 𝔤\mathfrak{g} is the (strict) Lie 2-algebra equivalently given as

  • the dg-Lie algebra of contractions ι x\iota_x and inner derivations ℒ x=[d CE,x]\mathcal{L}_x = [d_{CE},x] acting on the Chevalley-Eilenberg algebra of 𝔤\mathfrak{g};

  • the differential crossed module (𝔤→Id𝔤)(\mathfrak{g} \stackrel{Id}{\to} \mathfrak{g}) with the action being the adjoint action of 𝔤\mathfrak{g} on itself;

  • the dual of the Weil algebra W(𝔤)W(\mathfrak{g}).

In the first formulation this may be identified with the dg-Lie algebra whose

  • elements in degree -1 are the contractions ι x:CE(𝔤)→CE(𝔤)\iota_x : CE(\mathfrak{g}) \to CE(\mathfrak{g}) with x∈𝔤x \in \mathfrak{g};

  • elements in degree 0 are the inner derivations ℒ x=[d CE(𝔤),ι x]:CE(𝔤)→CE(𝔤)\mathcal{L}_x = [d_{CE(\mathfrak{g})}, \iota_x] : CE(\mathfrak{g}) \to CE(\mathfrak{g});

  • the differential ∂:𝔤→𝔤\partial : \mathfrak{g} \to \mathfrak{g} is given by the commutator ∂=[d CE(𝔤),−]\partial = [d_{CE(\mathfrak{g})}, -];

  • the bracket is the graded commutator bracket of derivations:

    • [ι x,ι y]=0[\iota_x, \iota_y] = 0

    • [ℒ x,ι y]=ι [x,y][\mathcal{L}_x, \iota_y] = \iota_{[x,y]}

    • [ℒ x,ℒ y]=ℒ [x,y][\mathcal{L}_x, \mathcal{L}_y] = \mathcal{L}_{[x,y]}.

So this is the full subalgebra of the automorphism ∞-Lie algebra of CE(𝔤)CE(\mathfrak{g}) on the inner derivations.

See Weil algebra as CE-algebra of inner derivations for more details.

Properties

  • The first formulation makes manifest that inn(𝔤)inn(\mathfrak{g}) is the structure that has historically been called Cartan calculus.

  • In the (∞,1)-category of ∞-Lie algebras inn(𝔤)inn(\mathfrak{g}) is equivalent to the point. See Weil algebra for details on this.
