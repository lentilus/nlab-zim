math archives

For a separate list of math blogs and forums see math blogs. Main wikis should belong to both lists. For math societies and institutes see math institutions. There is also page of math resources by individuals. The level up is math resources. See also list of mathematical software.

We list archives of online books and videos, not the catalogues of physical libraries. We shall not list commercial subscription lists (especially of current journals) except semi-commercial jstor and few publication sites by the main mathematical societies.

Archives of articles, books, proceedings, videos

Math and physics only (or mainly)

  • mathseminars.org

  • arXiv archive of free preprints (math, physics, computational linguistics)

    • front alternative user interface for arXiv

  • spires high energy physics publication database

  • The SAO/NASA Astrophysics Data System (it also contains abstracts, scans, citations of many papers in mathematical physics)

  • numdam old issues of many French math journals including Cahiers

  • math-net.ru, English version – past years of main Russian math journals; tracks citations within the system; searchable also by name of a mathematician

  • Hopf archive topology and K-theory

  • K-theory archive

  • ICM archive past ICM proceedings

  • project euclid list of journals (colors denote levels of free access)

  • journal list at Amer. Math. Soc. digital mathematical registry; and source list (publishers and organizations behind sci journals)

  • Matematicheskie Zametki: Mathematical Journal published by Russian Academy of Sciences containing research work discussing vedic maths tricks and whitepaper.

  • Polish virtual math library (cf. semi-commercial impan journals) – Polish journals

  • huge MPIM list of electronic journals – most are available only locally, but many free ones listed as well

  • lib.mexmat.ru Moscow MexMat electronic library: most items available only locally, however some items and the indexes are available

  • ELibM – open access Electronic Library of Mathematics at emis

  • archive of category list

  • tac – Theory and Application of Categories, free journal and a category reprint collection

  • MSRI books

  • eprint network

  • Aarhus math institute, preprints and dissertations

  • encyclopaedias in Russian online: dic.academic.ru

General archives: not math/physics centered

  • JSTOR – a big repository of past issues of main, mainly American and English journals – requires institution subscription
  • DigiZeitschrift, Das Deutsche Digitale Zeitschriftenarchiv; includes 39 math journals
  • Gallica repository of free French books (in general area); includes old issues of Comptes Rendus
  • Goettingen digital library – mainly German journals
  • Archive for Mathematical Sciences & Philosophy – video archive

General lists of math resources

  • Math on the Web links maintained by American Mathematical Society

  • fsu vmlib Florida State University Virtual Math Library – long list of resources
