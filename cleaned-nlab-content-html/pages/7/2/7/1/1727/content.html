2-category of 2-dimensional cobordisms

This is a special case of the (∞,n)(\infty,n)-category of cobordisms.

References

A complete generators and relations presentation of the 2-dimensional extended unoriented oriented bordism bicategory as a symmetric monoidal bicategory is given in

  • Chris Schommer-Pries, The classification of two-dimensional extended topological field theories PhD thesis, UC Berkeley (2009) (pdf)
