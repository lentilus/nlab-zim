Definition

Let ℕ *\mathbb{N}^* denote the list of natural numbers. A bar is a subset P⊆ℕ *P \subseteq \mathbb{N}^* such that for all sequences α\alpha of natural numbers, there exists a natural number nn such that the finite list of all α(i)\alpha(i) for all i<ni \lt n is in PP.

A bar PP is an inductive bar if for all lists a∈ℕ *a \in \mathbb{N}^* and all natural numbers n∈ℕn \in \mathbb{N}, if the concatenation of aa with nn is in PP, then aa is in PP.

A bar PP is a monotone bar if for all lists a,b∈ℕ *a, b \in \mathbb{N}^*, if aa is in PP, then the concatenation of aa and bb is in PP.
