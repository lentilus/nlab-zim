Idea

Derived differential geometry is higher differential geometry in an ambient (∞,1)-topos which is not 1-localic. This is the derived geometry corresponding to differential geometry. Typically this is specifically taken to be the derived geometry induced by the Lawvere theory for smooth algebras (C ∞C^\infty-rings):
