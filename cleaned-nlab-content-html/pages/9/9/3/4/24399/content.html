Overview

LOOPS’22 is a theoretical physics conference held in Lyon, France between 18 July and 22 July 2022. This conference consists of a variety of subjects at the frontier of theoretical physics, such as cosmology, holography, black hole physics, loop quantum gravity, and other topics in quantum field theory and quantum gravity. The website for this conference could be found here.
