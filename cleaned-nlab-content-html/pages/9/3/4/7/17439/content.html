Idea

The generalization of the Adams spectral sequence (Adams-Novikov spectral sequence) from stable homotopy theory to equivariant stable homotopy theory.

one may set up an equivariant analogue of the Adams spectral sequence [...][...] We prefer not write [[this]] out because this method needs time to mature. (Adams-Gunawardena-Miller 85)
