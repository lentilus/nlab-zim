Idea

An nn-topos is an n-category analog of a topos.

An nn-topos that is an (n,1)-category, hence where all k-morphisms for k≥2k \geq 2 are equivalences is called an (n,1)-topos. See there for more.

Examples

For every nn, The canonical (n+1)(n+1)-topos is nCat?, the (n+1)-category of n-categories.
