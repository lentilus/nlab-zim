Selected writings

Early discussion of connected objects (then called Z-objects):

  • Rudolf-E. Hoffmann: A categorical concept of connectedness. Résumés Coll. Amiens. Cahiers XIV-2 (1973) 34-35 [pdf, numdam:CTGDC_1973__14_2_153_0]

Early discussion of the notion of center of a category:

  • Rudolf-E. Hoffmann, Über das Zentrum einer Kategorie, Math. Nachr. 68 (1975) 299-306 [doi:10.1002/mana.19750680122]

On adjoint strings of functors:

  • Rudolf-E. Hoffmann, Sequences of adjoints for Ens-valued functors, manuscripta mathematica 32 (1980) [EuDML;154724]

On the relationship between cones and factorisation systems:

  • Rudolf-E. Hoffmann, Factorization of cones, Mathematische Nachrichten 87 1 (1979) 221-238. [doi:10.1002/mana.19790870120]

  • Rudolf-E. Hoffmann, Factorization of cones II, with applications to weak Hausdorff spaces, in: Categorical Aspects of Topology and Analysis: Proceedings of an International Conference Held at Carleton University, Ottawa, August 11–15, 1981, Springer (2006) [doi:10.1007/BFb0092878]
