Selected writings

Prediction of the omega-meson:

  • Yoichiro Nambu: Possible Existence of a Heavy Neutral Meson, Phys. Rev. 106 (1957) 1366 [doi:10.1103/PhysRev.106.1366]

Introducing discussion of what came to be called the Nambu-Goto action for strings (then motivated as explanation for the “dual resonance model” for hadrons, cf. Polyakov gauge-string duality):

  • Yoichiro Nambu, Duality and Hadrodynamics, Notes prepared for the Copenhagen High Energy Symposium (1970) [doi:10.1142/9789812795823_0026, pdf]

Precursor discussion to what came to be called the dual superconductor model of color confinement:

  • Yoichiro Nambu: Strings, monopoles, and gauge fields, Phys. Rev. D 10 (1974) 4262 [doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.10.4262]
