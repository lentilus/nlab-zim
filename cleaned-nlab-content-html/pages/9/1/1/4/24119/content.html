Idea

The notion of coexponential objects is dual to that of exponential objects.

Definition

Let XX and YY be objects of a category CC such that all binary coproducts with YY exist. (Usually, CC actually has all finite coproducts.) Then an coexponential object is an object Coexp(Y,X)Coexp(Y, X) equipped with a coevaluation map η:X→Coexp(Y,X)∐Y\eta \colon X \to Coexp(Y, X) \coprod Y which is universal in the sense that, given any object ZZ and map e:X→Z∐Ye\colon X \to Z \coprod Y, there exists a unique map u:Coexp(Y,X)→Zu\colon Coexp(Y, X) \to Z such that e=(u,id Y)∘ηe = (u, id_Y) \circ \eta.
