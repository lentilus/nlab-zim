Idea

The n-sphere of dimension n=6n = 6.

Properties

Coset structure

The 6-sphere, as a smooth manifold is diffeomorphic to the coset space

S 6≃G 2/SU(3) S^6 \simeq G_2/ SU(3)

of G₂ (automorphism group of the octonions) by SU(3) (Fukami-Ishihara 55).

For more see at G₂/SU(3) is the 6-sphere.

The induced action of G₂ on S 6S^6 induces an almost Hermitian structure which makes it a nearly Kaehler manifold?.

Complex structure — The Hopf problem

A famous open problem — known as the Hopf problem, after Heinz Hopf 1947 — was the question whether the 6-sphere admits an actual complex structure.

For review see Bryant 2014.

An AI-generated proof proposal of the existence of such complex structure was presented by Alpöge 2026 and was then claimed by Alexeev 2026 to have been verified via the Lean proof assistant.
