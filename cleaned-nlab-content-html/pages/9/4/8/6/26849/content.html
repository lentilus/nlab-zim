Definition

The Hantzsche-Wendt manifold (also called HW manifold or didicosm) is the coset space

D≔T 3/(ℤ 2×ℤ 2), D \coloneqq T^3/(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2) \,,

namely the quotient space of the 3-torus T 3T^3 by the antipodal action of ℤ 2×ℤ 2\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2 on two components. It is a compact, orientable and flat 3-manifold.

Properties

  • The holonomy group of the Hantzsche-Wendt manifold is ℤ 2 2\mathbb{Z}_2^2. (Miatello & Rossetti 99)
  • The first homology group of the Hantzsche-Wendt manifold is ℤ 4 2\mathbb{Z}_4^2. (Conway & Rossetti 03, p. 23, Egan 23)

Generalizations

The first and second amphidicosm are compact, non-orientable and flat 33-manifolds with holonomy group ℤ 2 2\mathbb{Z}_2^2 and first Betti number 11.

A generalized Hantzsche-Wendt manifold is a compact and flat nn-manifold with holonomy group ℤ 2 n−1\mathbb{Z}_2^{n-1}.
