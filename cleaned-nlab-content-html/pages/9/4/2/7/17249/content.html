Idea

In categories of simplicial objects, like SimplicialSets, the sSet-valued hom-object between two objects is often called the function complex (“simplicial complex of functions from XX to YY”).

The function complex of two simplicial sets is the collection of maps, left homotopies and higher homotopies between them, all itself organized into a simplicial set.

More formally, SimplicialSets is a Cartesian monoidal category and the corresponding internal hom is what is traditionally known as the function complex.

Definition

General

Suppose 𝒞\mathcal{C} is a category that admits small coproducts.

Given simplicial objects X,A∈𝒞 Δ opX,A\in \mathcal{C}^{\Delta^{op}}, their function complex is the simplicial set

Hom Δ(X,A)∈sSet Hom_{\Delta}(X,A) \;\; \in \;\; sSet

whose set of nn-simplices is the set of maps Δ n⊗A→B\Delta^n\otimes A\to B

(Hom Δ(X,A)) n=Hom 𝒞(Δ n⊗X,A), \big( Hom_{\Delta}(X,A) \big)_n \;\; = \;\; Hom_{\mathcal{C}} \big( \Delta^n \otimes X ,\, A \big) \,,

where ⊗\otimes denotes the copowering of simplicial objects over simplicial sets given by

(S⊗X) n=∐ i∈S nX n. (S \otimes X)_n \,=\, \coprod_{i \in S_n} X_n \,.

In simplicial sets

Specializing the general definition above to 𝒞=\mathcal{C} = sSet itself, the required tensoring is just the product of simplicial sets and so

Definition


The function complex between simplicial sets X,AX, A is the cartesian internal hom given by

X,A∈sSet⊢{Maps(X,A)∈sSet Maps(X,A) •=Hom sSet(Δ[•]×X,A) X, A \,\in\, sSet \;\;\;\; \vdash \;\;\;\; \left\{ \, \begin{array}{l} Maps(X,A) \;\; \in \;\; sSet \\ Maps(X,A)_\bullet \;\; = \;\; Hom_{sSet}\big( \Delta[\bullet] \times X ,\, A \big) \end{array} \right.

(e.g. Goerss & Jardine (2009), I.5)

Properties

In simplicial sets

Proposition

(component formula for evaluation map on function complexes)
In SimplicialSets, the evaluation map of the function complex (from Def. ) — defined as the counit of the (X×(−))⊣Maps(X,−)\big(X \times (-)\big)\dashv Maps(X,-)-adjunction — is given by:

X×Maps(X,A) →ev A X A A X n×Maps(X,A) n⏞Hom(X×Δ[n],A) →(ev A X) n A n (x,f) ↦ f(x,ι n), \begin{array}{cc} X \times Maps(X,A) &\xrightarrow{\;\; ev^X_A \;\;}& A \\ \phantom{A} \\ X_n \times \overset{ \mathclap{ Hom(X \times \Delta[n], A) } }{ \overbrace{ Maps(X,A)_n } } &\xrightarrow{\; \big(ev^X_A\big)_n \;}& A_n \\ \big( x ,\, f \big) &\mapsto& f(x, \iota_n) \,, \end{array}

where

ι n≔id Δ[n]∈Hom sSet(Δ[n],Δ[n])≃(Δ[n]) n \iota_n \;\coloneqq\; id_{\Delta[n]} \;\in\; Hom_{sSet}\big( \Delta[n],\, \Delta[n] \big) \;\simeq\; (\Delta[n])_n

denotes the unique non-degenerate nn-cell inside Δ[n]\Delta[n].

(e.g. Goerss & Jardine (2009), p. 20)

Proposition

(simplicial nerve intertwines function complexes with functor categories)

Let 𝒳,𝒜\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{A} be small strict categories and write Maps(𝒳,𝒜)Maps(\mathcal{X}, \mathcal{A}) for their functor category. Then under the simplicial nerve N:Cat sm⟶sSet N \;\colon\; Cat^{sm} \longrightarrow sSet this is isomorphic to the function complex (Def. ) between their separate simplicial nerves:

N(Maps(𝒳,𝒜))≃Maps(N(𝒳),N(𝒜)). N \big( Maps(\mathcal{X},\,\mathcal{A}) \big) \;\simeq\; Maps \big( N(\mathcal{X}) ,\, N(\mathcal{A}) \big) \,.

(For the proof see here at nerve.)

Examples

General

Example

(simplicial mapping complex between nerves of groupoids) For N(𝒢 i)N(\mathcal{G}_i) the nerves of groupoids, their simplicial mapping complex is isomorphic to the nerve of the functor groupoid from 𝒢 1\mathcal{G}_1 to 𝒢 2\mathcal{G}_2:

[N(𝒢 1),N(𝒢 2)]≃N(Func(𝒢 1,𝒢 2))∈SimplicialSets \big[ N(\mathcal{G}_1), \, N(\mathcal{G}_2) \big] \;\; \simeq \;\; N \big( Func(\mathcal{G}_1, \, \mathcal{G}_2) \big) \;\;\; \in \; SimplicialSets

Nerves of inertia groupoids

Example

(evaluation map on function complexes which are nerves of inertia groupoids)

Consider a discrete group GG. We write BG\mathbf{B}G for its delooping groupoid, here specifically for its strict version BG≔(G⇉*)\mathbf{B}G \,\coloneqq\,\big(G \rightrightarrows \ast \big). The nerve of this groupoid is the canonical model for the simplicial classifying space: NBG=W¯G. N \mathbf{B}G \,=\, \overline{W}G \,. The functor groupoid

ΛBG≔Maps(Bℤ,BG) \Lambda \mathbf{B}G \;\coloneqq\; Maps\big( \mathbf{B}\mathbb{Z} ,\, \mathbf{B}G \big)
