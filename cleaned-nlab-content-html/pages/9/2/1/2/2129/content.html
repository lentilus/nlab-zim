Definition

A vector space VV over a field kk is symplectic if it is equipped with an exterior 2-form ω∈Λ k 2V\omega \in \Lambda^2_k V such that ω ∧n=ω∧ω∧⋯∧ω\omega^{\wedge n}=\omega\wedge\omega\wedge\cdots\wedge\omega has the maximal rank.

A subspace W⊂VW\subset V in a symplectic vector space is isotropic if ω(v,v)=0\omega(v,v) = 0 for all v∈Wv\in W and Lagrangean (or lagrangian) if it is maximal isotropic (not proper subspace in any isotropic subspace). See wikipedia.
