Selected writings:

The origin of the idea of supergravity is attributed to:

  • Dmitry Volkov, Vladimir P. Akulov: Possible universal neutrino interaction, JETP Lett. 16 (1972) 438–440, Pisma Zh. Eksp. Teor. Fiz. 16 (1972) 621–624 [inSpire:76156, pdf, pdf]

followed up by the first model of supergravity (in nonlinear realization) constructed in

  • Dmitry Volkov, Vyacheslav Soroka: Higgs effect for Goldstone particles with spin 1/21/2, JETP Lett. 18 (1973) 312–314, Pisma Zh. Eksp. Teor. Fiz. 18 (1973) 529–532[inSpire:84359, pdf, pdf]

On D=4 supergravity formulated in superspace:

  • Vladimir Akulov, Dmitry Volkov, Vyacheslav Soroka: Generally covariant theories of gauge fields on superspace, Theor. Math. Phys. 31 (1977) 285 [doi:10.1007/BF01041233]

On Freund-Rubin compactification of D=11 and D=10 supergravity:

  • Dmitri P. Sorokin, Vladimir I. Tkach, Dmitrij V. Volkov, On the relationship between compactified vacua of D=11D=11 and D=10D=10 supergravities, Phys. Lett. B 161 (1985) 301-306 [doi:10.1016/0370-2693(85)90766-X]

Om the super particle and the spinning particle:

  • Dmitri P. Sorokin, Vladimir I. Tkach, Dmitrij V. Volkov, Aleksandr A. Zheltukhin, From the Superparticle Siegel Symmetry to the Spinning Particle Proper Time Supersymmetry, Phys. Lett. B 216 (1989) 302-306 [doi:10.1016/0370-2693(89)91119-2]

On the history of supergravity:

  • Dmitry Volkov, Supergravity before and after 1976. The story of goldstonions, in S. Duplij, J. Bagger, W. Siegel (eds): Concise Encyclopedia of Supersymmetry, Springer, 2004 [arXiv:hep-th/9404153]

On kappa-symmetry explained via the superembedding approach:

  • Dmitri Sorokin, Vladimir Tkach, Dmitrij Volkov, Superparticles, twistors and Siegel symmetry, Mod. Phys. Lett. A 4 10 (1989) 901-908 [spire:271923, doi:10.1142/S0217732389001064]

On superstrings and super membranes formulated in superspace:

  • Igor Bandos, Dmitrij Sorokin, Dmitrij Volkov, On the generalized action principle for superstrings and supermembranes, Phys. Lett. B 352 (1995) 269-275 [arXiv:hep-th/9502141]

On the derivation of the equations of motion for the superstring and the M2-brane via the superembedding approach:

  • Igor Bandos, Paolo Pasti, Dmitri Sorokin, Mario Tonin, Dmitry Volkov, Superstrings and supermembranes in the doubly supersymmetric geometrical approach, Nucl. Phys. B446:79-118, 1995 (arXiv:hep-th/9501113)
