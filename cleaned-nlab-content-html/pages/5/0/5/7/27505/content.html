Idea

The annular braid group or circlular braid group CBr nCBr_n (cf. Bellingeri & Bodin 2014 p 1) on n∈ℕn \in \mathbb{N} strands is the surface braid group of the annulus, hence the fundamental group of the configuration space of n n -points inside the annulus.

Properties

Definition

The affine type A braid group Br n affBr_n^{aff} has generators and relations given by the same formulas as for the Artin presentation of the ordinary braid group Br nBr_n, except that the indices in these formulas are identified modulo nn.

Proposition

The annular braid group is isomorphic to a semidirect product

(1)CBr n≃Br n aff⋊⟨c⟩⏟ℤ CBr_n \,\simeq\, Br_n^{aff} \rtimes \underset { \mathbb{Z} } { \underbrace{ \langle c \rangle } }

of the affine braid group (Def. ) generated by Artin Generators b ib_i with the group of integers, the latter generated by the braid cc which exhibits a 1-step cyclic permutation around the inner boundary of the annulus.

(Kent & Pfeifer 2002 Thm 1, cf. Gadbled, Thiel & Wagner 2015 Rem. 1.1).

Remark

The mapping class group of the nn-punctured annulus the further semidirect product of (1) with another copy of ℤ\mathbb{Z}:

MCG(𝔸,n)≃CBr n⋊ℤ≃Br n aff⋊ℤ 2. MCG(\mathbb{A}, n) \,\simeq\, CBr_n \rtimes \mathbb{Z} \,\simeq\, Br_n^{aff} \rtimes \mathbb{Z}^2 \,.

(Gadbled, Thiel & Wagner 2015 Rem. 1.2)
