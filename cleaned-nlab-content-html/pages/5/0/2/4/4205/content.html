Idea

A modular operad (Getzler & Kapranov 1998) is an operad that has the kind of extra structure which is exhibited by the operad whose nn-ary operations are Riemann surfaces with (n+1)(n+1)-punctures, and whose composition is sewing of such surfaces. Every modular operad has a structure of a cyclic operad and further additional structure.

Motivations

  • 2d TQFT

  • constructing graph complexes of Kontsevich as Feynman transforms of suitable twisted modular operads
