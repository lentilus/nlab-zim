Yakov Eliashberg

Yakov Eliashberg (Яков Матвеевич Элиашберг) is a Russian-American mathematician who has made contributions to contact and symplectic topology. He is currently the Herald L. and Caroline L. Ritch Professor of Mathematics at Stanford.
