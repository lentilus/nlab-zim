formal dg-algebra

This entry is about quasi-isomorphism to cochain cohomology. For infinitesimal thickening of smooth manifolds see at formal smooth manifold.

Definition

A dg-algebra AA is a formal dg-algebra if it is quasi-isomorphic (i.e.: isomorphic in the homotopy category of the model structure on dg-algebras)

A→≃H •(A) A \xrightarrow{\;\;\; \simeq \;\;\;} H^\bullet(A)

to its chain (co)homology regarded as a dg-algebra with trivial differential.

Since all dg-algebras are fibrant in the standard model structure, this is equivalent to the existence of a span

(1)A←≃ wQA→≃H •(A) A \overset{\;\; \simeq_w \;\;}{\leftarrow} Q A \overset{\;\; \simeq \;\;}{\rightarrow} H^\bullet(A)

of quasi-isomorphisms of dg-algebras.

Examples

In rational homotopy theory rational topological spaces are encoded in their Sullivan dg-algebras (cf. The fundamental theorem of dg-algebraic rational homotopy theory). A simply connected topological space XX whose Sullivan dg-algebra Ω •(X)\Omega^\bullet(X) is formal is called a formal topological space. (One can also say a formal rational space, to distinguish from the unrelated formal spaces in formal topology.) Such a space represents a formal homotopy type.

Examples:

  • compact Kähler manifolds (e.g. smooth projective varieties),

  • Lie groups,

  • classifying spacesBG\;B G of Lie groups GG,

  • some homogeneous spaces G/HG/H (see (Amann 12) for nonformal examples),

  • the unstable Thom spaces MU nM U_n and MSO nM S O_n,

  • the Eilenberg-MacLane space K(ℤ,n)K(\mathbb{Z},n),

    whose Sullivan minimal model is the dg-algebra on a single degree=n=n generator with trivial differential.,

  • the little n-disk operad is formal.
