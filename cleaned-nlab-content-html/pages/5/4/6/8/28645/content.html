Egan-Drozdov inequality

Contents

  • Statement
  • History
  • References

Statement

For two n−1n-1-spheres with respective radii RR and rr (with R≫rR\gg r) as well as distance dd between their centers, a nn-dimensional simplex fits inbetween them (meaning it’s fully inside the sphere with radius RR and fully outside the sphere with radius rr) if and only if:

d 2≤(R−(n−2)r)(R+nr)=R 2+2Rr−(n−2)nr 2. d^2 \leq(R-(n-2)r)(R+nr) =R^2+2Rr-(n-2)nr^2.

The Egan-Drozdov inequality is part of three results convering the case in different dimensions:

  • n=2n=2: Euler's theorem in geometry
  • n=3n=3: Grace-Danielsson inequality
  • n≥4n\geq 4: Egan-Drozdov inequality

History

Greg Egan conjectured the above result in Baez 14. In comments lost during the website being moved, he proceeded to show the inequality was sufficient in 2018. Sergei Drozdov showed the inequality was also necessary in Drozdov 25.
