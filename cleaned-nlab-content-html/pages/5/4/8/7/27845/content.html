Idea

In electromagnetism, the standard flux quantization condition (“Dirac charge quantization”) implies that the magnetic flux Φ Σ\Phi_\Sigma through a closed surface Σ\Sigma (which may be the one-point compactification of an open surface if flux is constrained to vanish at infinity) is an integer multiple N∈ℕN \in \mathbb{N} of an indecomposable quantum of magnetic flux Φ 0\Phi_0:

Φ Σ=N⋅Φ 0. \Phi_\Sigma \;=\; N \cdot \Phi_0 \,.

Individual magnetic flux quanta are directly observable in experiment:

  • in the guise of Abrikosov vortices for magnetic flux through effectively 2-dimensional type II superconductors

    (since the elementary charge carries in this case are Cooper pairs of electrons what is called the flux quantum for superconductors is in fact 12Φ 0\tfrac{1}{2}\Phi_0)

  • in the guise of solitonic anyon quasi-particles for magnetic flux through effectively 2-dimensional fractional quantum Hall systems

and, hypothetically (conditioned on the existence of magnetic monopoles):

  • in the guise of flux through any 2-sphere surrounding a magnetic monopole.

Definition

The quantum of magnetic flux is

ϕ 0≔he, \phi_0 \;\coloneqq\; \frac{h}{e} \,,

where:

  • hh is Planck's constant

  • ee is the charge of the electron.

In SI units this is approximately

ϕ 0 ≡he ∼6.626×10 −34Js1.602×10 −19C ∼4.136×10 −15JsC =4.136×10 −15Tm 2, \begin{aligned} \phi_0 &\equiv\; \frac{h}{e} \\ &\sim\; \frac {6.626 \times 10^{-34} \, J s} {1.602 \times 10^{-19} \, C} \\ &\sim\; 4.136 \times 10^{-15} \, \frac{J s}{C} \\ &=\; 4.136 \times 10^{-15} \, T m^2 \,, \end{aligned}

where in the last step we used that

one Joule is one Newton meter

J=Nm J \;=\; N m

and one Tesla is one Newton second per Coulomb meter

T≔NsCm. T \;\coloneqq\; \frac { N s } { C m } \,.
