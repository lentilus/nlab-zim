Idea

A concept of 22-Hilbert spaces is supposed to be a categorification of that of Hilbert spaces, or at least of finite dimensional such: inner product spaces.

One way to define this is as a Kapranov-Voevodsky 2-vector space where the hom-functor plays the role of the categorified inner product (Baez 97).

In other words, a 22-Hilbert space can be defined as an abelian H * H^\ast -category.
