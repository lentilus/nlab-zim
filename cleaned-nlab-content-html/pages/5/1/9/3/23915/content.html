Idea

A version of the k-tuply monoidal n-groupoid but for A n A_n spaces.

Definition

Homotopy-theoretic

Invoking the homotopy hypothesis, we define a kk-tuply associative nn-groupoid to be an A kA_k-nn-truncated type: a topological space which is a homotopy n-type with a multiplication that is associative up to higher homotopies involving up to nn variables.
