Definition

Let 𝒜\mathcal{A} be an abelian category with translation.

An object II in the category of chain complexes modulo chain homotopy, K(𝒜)K(\mathcal{A}), is homotopically injective if for every X∈K(𝒜)X \in K(\mathcal{A}) that is quasi-isomorphic to 00 we have

Hom K(𝒜)(X,I)≃0. Hom_{K(\mathcal{A})}(X,I) \simeq 0 \,.

Let

QuasiIsoMono={f∈Mor(A c)|fmonoandquasiio} QuasiIsoMono = \{f \in Mor(A_c) | f mono and quasiio\}

be the set of morphisms in the category of chain complexes Ch •(𝒜)Ch_\bullet(\mathcal{A}) which are both quasi-isomorphisms as well as monomorphisms.

Then

A complex II is an injective object with respect to monomorphic quasi-isomorphisms precisely if

  • it is homotopically injective in the sense of complexes in 𝒜\mathcal{A}; and

  • it is injective as an object of 𝒜\mathcal{A} (with respect to morphisms f:X→Yf : X \to Y such that 0→X→fY0 \to X \stackrel{f}{\to} Y is exact).

Properties

In complexes in a Grothendieck category

Proposition For 𝒜\mathcal{A} a Grothendieck category with translation T:𝒜→𝒜T : \mathcal{A} \to \mathcal{A}, every complex XX in Ch •(𝒜)Ch_\bullet(\mathcal{A}) is quasi-isomorphic to a complex II which is injective and homotopically injective (i.e. QuasiIsoMono-injective).

Relation to derived categories

For 𝒜\mathcal{A} an abelian Grothendieck category with translation the full subcategory K hi(𝒜)⊂K(𝒜)K_{hi}(\mathcal{A}) \subset K(\mathcal{A}) of homotopically injective complexes realizes the derived category D(𝒜)D(\mathcal{A}) of 𝒜\mathcal{A}:

Q| K hi(A):K hi(A)→≃D(A), Q|_{K_{hi}(A)} : K_{hi}(A) \stackrel{\simeq}{\to} D(A) \,,

where Q:K(A)→D(A)Q : K(A) \to D(A) and Q| K hi(A)Q|_{K_{hi}(A)} has a right adjoint.

It follows that for DD any other triangulated category, every triangulated functor F:K(𝒜)→DF : K(\mathcal{A}) \to D has a right derived functor RF:D(𝒜)→DR F : D(\mathcal{A}) \to D which is computed by evaluating FF on injective replacements: for R:D(𝒜)→≃K hi(𝒜)R : D(\mathcal{A}) \stackrel{\simeq}{\to} K_{hi}(\mathcal{A}) a weak inverse to QQ, we have

RF≃D(A)→RK hi(A)↪K(A)→FD. R F \simeq D(A) \stackrel{R}{\to} K_{hi}(A) \hookrightarrow K(A) \stackrel{F}{\to} D \,.
