Lie-algebra valued differential 1-forms

Definition

Let GG be a Lie group, and write 𝔤\mathfrak{g} for its Lie algebra. The set of Lie algebra valued differential 1-forms is the tensor product

Ω 1(U,𝔤)=Ω 1(U)⊗ ℝ𝔤. \Omega^1(U,\mathfrak{g}) = \Omega^1(U) \otimes_{\mathbb{R}} \mathfrak{g} \,.

flat forms:

Ω flat 1(U,𝔤)={ω∈Ω 1(U,𝔤)|F ω=dω+[ω,ω]=0}. \Omega^1_{flat}(U, \mathfrak{g}) = \left\{ \omega \in \Omega^1(U,\mathfrak{g}) | F_\omega = \mathbf{d} \omega + [\omega, \omega] = 0 \right\} \,.
