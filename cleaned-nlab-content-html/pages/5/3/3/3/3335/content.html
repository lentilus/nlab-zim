  • Kathryn Hess, Magdalena Kedziorek, Emily Riehl, Brooke Shipley, A necessary and sufficient condition for induced model structures, J. Topology 10 2 (2017) 324-369 [arXiv:1509.08154, doi:10.1112/topo.12011]
