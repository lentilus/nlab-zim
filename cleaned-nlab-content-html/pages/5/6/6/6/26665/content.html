Idea

BSU(n)B SU(n) is the classifying space for (principal bundles with structure group) the special unitary group SU(n)SU(n). Important examples are principal SU(2)-bundles, principal SU(3)-bundles and principal SU(4)-bundles.

Definition

Definition

BSU(n)B SU(n) is the limit of the sequence of canonical inclusions of complex orientable Grassmannians Gr˜ n(ℂ k)↪Gr˜ n(ℂ k+1)\widetilde{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^k)\hookrightarrow\widetilde{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^{k+1}):

BSU(n)≔lim⟶ kGr˜ n(ℂ k) B SU(n) \coloneqq\underset{\longrightarrow}{\lim}_k\widetilde{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^k)

As the complex orientable Grassmannian can be written as a homogenous space by

Gr˜ n(ℂ k)=SU(k)/(SU(n)×SU(k−n)) \widetilde{Gr}_n(\mathbb{C}^k) =SU(k)/(SU(n)\times SU(k-n))

the group structure carries over to BSU(n)B SU(n).

Smallest classifying space

Since SU(1)≅1SU(1)\cong 1 is the trivial group, the classifying space BSU(1)B SU(1) is the trivial topological space. Since SU(2)≅Sp(1)SU(2)\cong Sp(1), once has

BSU(2)≅BSp(1)≅ℍP ∞. B SU(2) \cong B Sp(1) \cong\mathbb{H}P^\infty.

This result is important for the classification of principal SU(2)-bundles.

Cohomology

Theorem

The cohomology ring of BSU(n)B SU(n) with coefficients in the ring ℤ\mathbb{Z} of integers is generated by the Chern classes and given by

H *(BSU(n);ℤ)≅ℤ[c 2,…,c n]. H^*(B SU(n);\mathbb{Z}) \cong\mathbb{Z}[c_2,\ldots,c_n].

(Hatcher 02, Example 4D.7.)

Colimit

The canonical inclusions SU(n)↪SU(n+1)S U(n)\hookrightarrow S U(n+1) yield canonical inclusions BSU(n)↪BSU(n+1)B S U(n)\hookrightarrow B S U(n+1) of their respective classifying spaces. The colimit is denoted as

BSU≔lim⟶ nBSU(n). B SU \coloneqq\underset{\longrightarrow}{\lim}_n B SU(n).

and indeed the classifying space for SU≔lim⟶ nSU(n)S U\coloneqq\underset{\longrightarrow}{\lim}_n SU(n).
