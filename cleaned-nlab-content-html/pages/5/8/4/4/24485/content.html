Selected writings

Early discussion of (co)monads, their Kleisli categories and Eilenberg-Moore categories and given apparently the first statement of morphisms of monads:

  • Dieter Pumplün, Eine Bemerkung über Monaden und adjungierte Funktoren, Mathematische Annalen 185 (1970) 329-337 [eudml:161964, pdf]

On totally convex spaces:

  • Dieter Pumplün, Helmut Röhrl: Banach Spaces and totally convex spaces I, Comm. Alg. 12 8 (1984) 953-1019 [doi:10.1080/00927878408823035]

  • Dieter Pumplün, Helmut Röhrl, Banach Spaces and totally convex spaces II, Comm. Alg. 13 5 (1985) 1047-1113 [doi:10.1080/00927878508823205]

On totally convex algebras:

  • Dieter Pumplün, Helmut Röhrl, Totally convex algebras, Comment. Math. Univ. Carolin. 33 2 (1992) 205–235
