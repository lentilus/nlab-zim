biactegory

Let CC be a monoidal category (in fact one can easily modify all statements to generalize all statements here to bicategories). One can consider several variants of the 2-category of all categories with monoidal action of CC, (co)lax monoidal functors and their transformations. A category with an action of CC is sometimes called a CC-actegory. The word ‘module category’ over CC is also used, especially when the category acted upon is in addition also additive, like the examples in representation theory.

If a CC-actegory is a categorification of a module, then for two monoidal categories CC and DD, we should categorify a bimodule, which we call CC-DD-biactegory, or sometimes still a CC-DD-bimodule.

The two actions on a usual bimodule commute; for biactegories the commuting is up to certain coherence laws, which are in fact the expression of an invertible distributive law between the two monoidal actions. The tensor product of biactegories can be defined (here the invertibility of the distributive law is needed) as a bicoequalizer of a certain diagram.
