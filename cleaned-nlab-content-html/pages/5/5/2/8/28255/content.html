Idea

A 2-oriented bordism is a B-bordism for the tangential structure ((B,f)-structure) being the 2-orientation. Its bordism homology theory and cobordism cohomology theory are described by the Thom spectrum M2-Orient. Its definition is fully analogous to that of similar bordisms like oriented bordism. Similarily, there are 2-oriented bordism groups and the 2-oriented bordism ring:

Ω n 2-Orient≔π nM2-Orient=lim k→∞π n+kM2-Orient k; \Omega_n^{2\text{-}Orient} \coloneqq\pi_n M2\text{-}Orient =\lim_{k\rightarrow\infty}\pi_{n+k} M2\text{-}Orient_k;
Ω 2-Orient≔⨁ n∈ℕΩ n 2-Orient. \Omega^{2\text{-}Orient} \coloneqq\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbb{N}}\Omega_n^{2\text{-}Orient}.

Properties

Proposition

Every nn-dimensional 2-orient manifold is 2-orient bordant to a min{9,⌈n2−1⌉}\min\left\{9,\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}-1\right\rceil\right\}-connected 2-orient manifold, equivalently meaning that every 2-orient bordism homology class in Ω n 2-Orient\Omega_n^{2\text{-}Orient} can be represented by such a 2-orient manifold. (For n≥19n\geq 19, the result stabilizes at a 9-connected 2-orient manifold.)

(Botvinnik & Labbi 14, Lem. 3.2 (1))

Proposition

For nn-dimensional min{9,⌈n2−1⌉}\min\left\{9,\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}-1\right\rceil\right\}-connected 2-orient manifolds MM and NN, a 2-orient bordism W:M⇝NW\colon M\rightsquigarrow N exists with M↪WM\hookrightarrow W also min{9,⌈n2−1⌉}\min\left\{9,\left\lceil\frac{n}{2}-1\right\rceil\right\}-connected.

(Botvinnik & Labbi 14, Lem. 3.2 (2))

Proposition

If a nn-dimensional kk-connected compact 2-orient manifold MM with k≤8k\leq 8 and n≥2k+3n\geq 2k+3 is 2-orient bordant to another compact 2-orient manifold NN, then MM can be obtained from NN by surgery of codimension at least k+2k+2.

(Botvinnik & Labbi 14, Prop. 3.4)
