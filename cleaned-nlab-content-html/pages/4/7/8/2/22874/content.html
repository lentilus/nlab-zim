On M-theory on S1/G_HW times H/G_ADE and D=6 N=(1,0) SCFT:

On 2-group higher symmetry:

On little string theory in heterotic string theory via F-theory-methods:

On QFT with defects:

On the Coulomb branch of D=3 N=4 SYM from heterotic string theory on ADE-singularities:
