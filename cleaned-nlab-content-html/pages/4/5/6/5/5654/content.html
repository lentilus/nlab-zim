Definition

Let CC be a stable (∞,1)-category (or (∞,1)-topos) and A∈CA \in C a stable coefficient object. For X∈CX \in C and n∈ℤn \in \mathbb{Z} or (n∈ℕn \in \mathbb{N}) write

H n(X,A):=π 0C(X,B nA) H^n(X,A) := \pi_0 C(X, \mathbf{B}^n A)

for the cohomology of XX with coefficients in AA in degree nn.

Say a morphism in CC is AA-local if it induces isomorphisms on all these cohomology groups. Let WW be the class of all such morphisms.

Then the AA-cohomology localization of CC is – if it exists – the localization of an (∞,1)-category of CC at WW.

Examples

  • At function algebras on ∞-stacks is discussed a class of examples of cohomology localizations of (∞,1)-sheaf (∞,1)-toposes over sites of algebras over an abelian Lawvere theory at cohomology with coefficients in the canonical line object 𝔸 1\mathbb{A}^1.
