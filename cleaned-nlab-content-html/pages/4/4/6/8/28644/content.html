Statement

For two 2-spheres with respective radii RR and rr (with R≫rR\gg r) as well as distance dd between their centers, a three-dimensional simplex fits inbetween them (meaning it’s fully inside the sphere with radius RR and fully outside the sphere with radius rr) if and only if:

d 2≤(R−r)(R+3r)=R 2+2Rr−3r 2 d^2 \leq (R-r)(R+3r) =R^2+2Rr-3r^2

The Grace-Danielsson inequality is part of three results convering the case in different dimensions:

  • n=2n=2: Euler's theorem in geometry
  • n=3n=3: Grace-Danielsson inequality
  • n≥4n\geq 4: Egan-Drozdov inequality

History

The Grace-Danielsson inequality was independently proved by John Hilton Grace in Grace 18 and Gösta Danielsson in Danielsson 52. A connection to quantum information theory was described in Milne 14.
