Idea

Am Iwasawa decomposition is an analytic diffeomorphism G≃K×A×NG \,\simeq\, K \times A \times N between

  1. a connected real semisimple Lie group GG

  2. a product manifold K×A×NK \times A \times N,

    where KK, AA and NN are

    • analytic\;subgroups of GG,

    • with AA and NN simply connected,

    • integratingLie algebras k,a,nk,a,n, respectively, where

    • k⊕pk\oplus p is the Cartan decomposition? of the Lie algebra of GG,

    • aa is the maximal Abelian subalgebra of pp

    • nn is a certain nilpotent Lie subalgebra of pp with p≃a⊕np \simeq a\oplus n as vector spaces.

The Iwasawa decomposition generalizes a statement expressing the QR decomposition of real matrices.
