Idea

A torus knot is a knot that can be drawn on the surface of a torus.

Definition

A knot KK is said to be a torus knot if it can be embedded in the surface of a torus, that is, we have the map K:S 1→ℝ 3K : S^1\to \mathbb{R}^3 factors through the embedding of some torus T 1≅S 1×S 1T_1\cong S^1\times S^1 into ℝ 3\mathbb{R}^3;
