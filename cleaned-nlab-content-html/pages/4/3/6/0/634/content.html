Definition

A 2-category KK has three different opposites (two independent):

  • The 11-cell dual K opK^{op}, in which the 11-cells but not the 22-cells are reversed.
  • The 22-cell dual K coK^{co}, in which the 22-cells but not the 11-cells are reversed.
  • The 11- and 22-cell dual K coopK^{co op} in which both 11-cells and 22-cells are reversed.

Interaction with 2-functor 2-categories

The category 2-Cat is cartesian closed, with the exponential B AB^A being given by the 2-functor 2-category [A,B][A, B] whose objects are (strict) 2-functors from AA to BB, whose 1-cells are 2-natural transformations, and whose 2-cells are modifications.

  • [A,B] op=[A op,B op][A, B]^{op} = [A^{op}, B^{op}]
  • [A,B] co=[A co,B co][A, B]^{co} = [A^{co}, B^{co}]
  • [A,B] coop=[A coop,B coop][A, B]^{co op} = [A^{co op}, B^{co op}]
