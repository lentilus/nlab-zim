superconformal super Lie group

Idea

A superconformal super Lie group is a Lie group analog of a super Lie algebra which extends the Lie algebra of a conformal group, hence the Lie integration of a superconformal super Lie algebra. See at Supersymmetry – Classification – Superconformal symmetry.

Related concepts

  • super Poincare group

  • super anti de Sitter group, super anti de Sitter spacetime

  • superconformal multiplet
