Idea

The Levin-Wen model is lattice model for certain phenomena in 2-dimensional solid state physics. It can be regarded as a 3d TQFT.

Related concepts

  • The 4d TQFT analog of the Levin-Wen model is the Walker-Wang model.

  • topological order

  • string-net model
