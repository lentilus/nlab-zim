Definition

A nilmanifold is a differentiable manifold which admits a smooth transitive action by a nilpotent Lie group GG, hence which is diffeomorphic to a coset space (homogeneous space) G/ΓG/\Gamma for Γ⊂G\Gamma \subset G a closed subgroup of a nilpotent Lie group.
