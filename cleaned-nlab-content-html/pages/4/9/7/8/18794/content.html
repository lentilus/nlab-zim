Definition

For RR some ring and N,N 1,…,N nN,N_1,\dots,N_n modules over this ring, a function

f:N 1×⋯×N n⟶N f \;\colon\; N_1 \times \cdots \times N_n \longrightarrow N

is homogeneous of degree kk if for all r∈Rr \in R we have

f(ra 1,⋯,ra n)=r kf(a 1,⋯,a n). f(r a_1, \cdots, r a_n) = r^k f(a_1, \cdots, a_n) \,.

  • quadratic form
