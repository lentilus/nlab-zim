Selected writings

On the AdS-CFT correspondence:

  • Ofer Aharony, Steven Gubser, Juan Maldacena, Hirosi Ooguri, Yaron Oz, Large NN Field Theories, String Theory and Gravity, Phys.Rept. 323 (2000) 183-386 [arXiv:hep-th/9905111]

and specifically for higher spin gauge theory:

  • Ofer Aharony, Shai Chester, Erez Urbach, A Derivation of AdS/CFT for Vector Models (arXiv:2011.06328)

On (p,q)5-brane webs:

  • Ofer Aharony, Amihay Hanany, Barak Kol, Webs of (p,q)(p,q) 5-branes, Five Dimensional Field Theories and Grid Diagrams, JHEP 9801:002, 1998 (arXiv:hep-th/9710116)

  • Ofer Aharony, Amihay Hanany, Branes, Superpotentials and Superconformal Fixed Points, Nucl. Phys. B504:239-271, 1997 (arXiv:hep-th/9704170)

On D-brane polarization in a context of AdS/QCD:

  • Ofer Aharony, Arvind Rajaraman, String Theory Duals for Mass-deformed SO(N)SO(N) and USp(2N)USp(2N) 𝒩=4\mathcal{N}=4 SYM Theories, Phys. Rev. D62:106002, 2000 (arXiv:hep-th/0004151)

On multi trace operators in AdS/CFT:

  • Ofer Aharony, Micha Berkooz, Eva Silverstein, Multiple-Trace Operators and Non-Local String Theories, JHEP 0108:006, 2001 (arXiv:hep-th/0105309)

  • Ofer Aharony, Micha Berkooz, Eva Silverstein, Non-local string theories on AdS 3AdS_3 times S 3S^3 and stable non-supersymmetric backgrounds, Phys. Rev. D65:106007, 2002 (arXiv:hep-th/0112178)

Early discussion towards holographic QCD:

  • Ofer Aharony, The non-AdS/non-CFT correspondence, or three different paths to QCD, in: Progress in string, field and particle theory, Springer, (2003) 3-24 [arXiv:hep-th/0212193, doi:10.1007/978-94-010-0211-0]

On D=2 Yang-Mills theory as the effective field theory of a string theory:

  • Ofer Aharony, Suman Kundu, Tal Sheaffer, A String Theory for Two Dimensional Yang-Mills Theory I [arXiv:2312.12266]
