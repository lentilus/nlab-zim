Idea

Rigs and rig homomorphisms form the category Rig.

Definition

We consider rigs as having an additive unit 00, a multiplicative unit 11 and being such that 0.x=x.0=00.x = x.0 = 0, as discussed in the entry rig.

We recall that a rig homomorphism f:R→Sf \colon R \rightarrow S is a function which is a monoid homomorphism for both the additive underlying monoid and the multiplicative underlying monoid.
