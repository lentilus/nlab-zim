Schauenburg bialgebroid

Motivation

Schauenburg bialgebroid, also called Ehresmann-Schauenburg bialgebroid, is a noncommutative generalization of the algebra of functions on an Atiyah groupoid (also called gauge or Ehresmann groupoid) of a principal bundle where the principal bundle is replaced by a Hopf-Galois extension (on the level of algebra of functions).

Definition

It is an associative bialgebroid whose structure, given a faithfully flat HH-Hopf-Galois extension U↪EU\hookrightarrow E, is described in Brzeziński-Wisbauer2003, 34.14. The description there is by first constructing an associated coring, the Ehresmann coring B=(E⊗ kE) coHB= (E\otimes_k E)^{co H} (which is a more general construction, defined for any coalgebra-Galois extension which is faithfully flat as a left module over the base of the extension), and then noticing that in this case BB is a subalgebra of E⊗ kE opE\otimes_k E^{op} and equipping it with source s:U→Es:U\to E, a↦a⊗1a\mapsto a\otimes 1 and target t:U→E opt:U\to E^{op}, a↦1⊗aa\mapsto 1\otimes a.
