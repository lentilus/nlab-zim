Idea

The differential equation encoded by a Dirac operator.

The equations of motion of the Dirac field.

Related concepts

  • Dirac spinor, Dirac conjugate

  • Dirac field

  • heat equation

  • wave equation, Klein-Gordon equation

  • Schrödinger equation
