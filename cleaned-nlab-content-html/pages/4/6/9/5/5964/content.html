Selected writings

On localizations of 2-categories via bicategories of fractions:

  • Dorette A. Pronk, Etendues and stacks as bicategories of fractions, Comp. Math. 102 3 (1996) pp.243-303. (numdam:CM_1996__102_3_243_0)

On orbifolds as Lie groupoids/differentiable stacks:

  • Ieke Moerdijk, Dorette Pronk: Orbifolds, sheaves and groupoids, K-theory 12 1 (1997) 3-21 [doi:10.1023/A:1007767628271]

and as topological groupoids/topological stacks:

  • Vesta Coufal, Dorette Pronk, Carmen Rovi, Laura Scull, Courtney Thatcher: Orbispaces and their Mapping Spaces via Groupoids: A Categorical Approach, in: Women in Topology: Collaborations in Homotopy Theory, Contemporary Mathematics 641 (2015) 135-166 [arXiv:1401.4772, doi:10.1090/conm/641]

On model structures on DblCat:

  • Thomas M. Fiore, Simona Paoli, Dorette A. Pronk, Model structures on the category of small double categories, Algebraic & Geometric Topology 8 (2008) 1855–1959 [doi:10.2140/agt.2008.8.1855]

On orbifold cohomology (such as orbifold K-theory):

  • Dorette Pronk, Laura Scull, Translation Groupoids and Orbifold Bredon Cohomology, Canad. J. Math. 62 (2010), 614-645 (arXiv:0705.3249, doi:10.4153/CJM-2010-024-1)

On exponential objects among internal groupoids in Top (topological groupoids):

  • Susan B. Niefield, Dorette A. Pronk, Internal groupoids and exponentiability, Cahiers de topologie et géométrie différentielle catégoriques, LX 4 (2019) (pdf)

On the equivariant fundamental groupoid of an orbifold:

  • Dorette Pronk, Laura Scull, The Equivariant Fundamental Groupoid as an Orbifold Invariant, Homology, Homotopy and Applications 23 1 (2021) (arXiv:1908.01201, doi:10.4310/HHA.2021.v23.n1.a3)
