Selected writings

Early discussion of abelian Chern-Simons theory as an effective field theory for “composite bosons” in the fractional quantum Hall effect:

  • Shou Cheng Zhang, T. H. Hansson S. Kivelson: Effective-Field-Theory Model for the Fractional Quantum Hall Effect, Phys. Rev. Lett. 62 (1989) 82 [doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.62.82]

Early review:

  • Shou Cheng Zhang: The Chern-Simons-Landau-Ginzburg theory of the fractional quantum Hall effect, International Journal of Modern Physics B 06 01 (1992) 25-58 [doi:10.1142/S0217979292000037, pdf]

Predicting a quantum spin Hall effect in topological insulators based on mercury telluride (HgTe quantum wells):

  • Andrei Bernevig, Shou-Cheng Zhang, Quantum Spin Hall Effect, Phys. Rev. Lett. 96, 106802 (2006) (arXiv:cond-mat/0504147, doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.106802)

  • Andrei Bernevig, Taylor Hughes, Shou-Cheng Zhang, Quantum spin Hall effect and topological phase transition in HgTe quantum wells, Science 15 December 2006: 314, n. 5806, pp. 1757-1761 (doi:10.1126/science.1133734, arXiv:cond-mat/0611399)

On topological phases of matter:

  • Shou Cheng Zhang: Viewpoint: Topological states of quantum matter, APS Physics 1 6 (2008) [doi:10.1103/Physics.1.6]

On topological insulators and topological superconductors:

  • Xiao-Liang Qi, Shou Cheng Zhang, Topological insulators and superconductors, Rev. Mod. Phys. 83 (2011) 1057-1110 [arXiv:1008.2026, doi:10.1103/RevModPhys.83.1057]

  • Jing Wang, Shou-Cheng Zhang, Topological states of condensed matter, Nature Materials 16 (2017) 1062–1067 [doi:10.1038/nmat5012]

On Chern-Simons theory potentially applicable to a form of Berry curvature on the Brillouin torus of 3d topological semimetals, with nodal lines playing the role of Wilson lines:

  • Biao Lian, Cumrun Vafa, Farzan Vafa, Shou-Cheng Zhang, Chern-Simons theory and Wilson loops in the Brillouin zone, Phys. Rev. B 95 (2017) 094512 [doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.95.094512]

On the quantum anomalous Hall effect:

  • Jing Wang, Biao Lian, Shou-Cheng Zhang: Quantum anomalous Hall effect in magnetic topological insulators, Physica Scripta 2015 T164 (2015) 014003 [arXiv:1409.6715, doi:10.1088/0031-8949/2015/T164/014003]

  • Chao-Xing Liu, Shou-Cheng Zhang, Xiao-Liang Qi: The Quantum Anomalous Hall Effect: Theory and Experiment, Annual Review of Condensed Matter Physics 7 (2016) [arXiv:1508.07106, doi:10.1146/annurev-conmatphys-031115-011417]
