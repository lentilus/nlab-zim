enriched preorder

Idea

Recall (here) that a preorder may be understood as a thin (0,1)-category, hence as a thin category enriched over the cartesian monoidal proset of truth values. In generalization, one may speak of enriching preorders over other monoidal posets.

Defintion

Let (M,≤,∧,⊤)(M, \leq, \wedge, \top) be a monoidal poset. A MM-enriched proset or proset enriched over/in MM is a set PP with a binary function o:P×P→Mo:P \times P \to M such that

  • for every a∈Pa \in P, b∈Pb \in P, and c∈Pc \in P, o(a,b)∧o(b,c)≤o(a,c)o(a, b) \wedge o(b, c) \leq o(a, c)

  • for every a∈Pa \in P, ⊤≤o(a,a)\top \leq o(a, a).

Examples

  • An ordinary poset is just an Ω\Omega-enriched poset, with Ω\Omega the set of truth values.

  • A Lawvere metric space is a ℝ ≥0¯\overline{\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}}-enriched proset, where ℝ ≥0¯\overline{\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}} are the non-negative extended Dedekind real numbers.

  • A quasipseudometric space is a ℝ ≥0\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}-enriched proset, where ℝ ≥0\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0} are the non-negative Dedekind real numbers.

  • A quasimetric space is a ℝ ≥0\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}-enriched poset, where ℝ ≥0\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0} are the non-negative Dedekind real numbers.

  • A pseudometric space is a ℝ ≥0\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}-enriched symmetric proset, where ℝ ≥0\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0} are the non-negative Dedekind real numbers.

  • A metric space is a ℝ ≥0\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}-enriched set, where ℝ ≥0\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0} are the non-negative Dedekind real numbers.

  • A set with an irreflexive comparison, such as an apartness relation or a linear order, is an Ω op\Omega^\op-enriched poset, where the co-Heyting algebra Ω op\Omega^\op is the opposite poset of the set of truth values Ω\Omega.
