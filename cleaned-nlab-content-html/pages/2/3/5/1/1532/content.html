An algebraic cycle on a scheme XX of finite type over a field kk is a finite linear combination ∑ i=1 rn iZ i\sum_{i=1}^r n_i Z_i of integral closed subschemes Z i⊂XZ_i\subset X with integral coefficients n in_i. Sometimes (for equidimensional XX) one looks at the grading by codimension 𝒵 *\mathcal{Z}_*.

Typical choices are rational, algebraic and numerical adequate equivalence relations. The rational is the finest and the numerical is the coarsest nonzero adequate equivalence relation.
