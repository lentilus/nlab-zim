George Janelidze

George Janelidze is a Georgian-South African category theorist, currently a professor at the University of Cape Town. He studied in Tbilisi under Hvedri Inassaridze with the dissertation “Satellites and Galois Extensions of Commutative Rings”.

His research includes nonabelian homological algebra, the discovery of categorical Galois theory, semiabelian categories, internal actions and internal crossed modules, categorical universal algebra etc.
