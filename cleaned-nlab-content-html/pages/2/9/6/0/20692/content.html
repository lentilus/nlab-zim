Selected writings

On D=11 N=1 supergravity solutions for the near horizon geometry of black M5-branes wrapped on supersymmetric cycles:

  • Jerome Gauntlett, Nakwoo Kim, Daniel Waldram, M-Fivebranes Wrapped on Supersymmetric Cycles, Phys. Rev. D63 (2001) 126001 (arXiv:hep-th/0012195)

Discussion of pp-wave spacetimes as Penrose limits of AdSp x S^q spacetimes and of the corresponding limit of the AdS5-CFT4 correspondence:

  • Nakwoo Kim, Ari Pankiewicz, Soo-Jong Rey, Stefan Theisen, Superstring on PP-Wave Orbifold from Large-N Quiver Gauge Theory, Eur. Phys. J. C25:327-332, 2002 (arXiv:hep-th/0203080)

On the BMN matrix model:

  • Nakwoo Kim, Jan Plefka, On the Spectrum of PP-Wave Matrix Theory, Nucl. Phys. B643:31-48, 2002 (arXiv:hep-th/0207034)

On the BMN matrix model as a KK-compactification of D=4 N=4 super Yang-Mills theory on the 3-sphere:

  • Nakwoo Kim, Thomas Klose, Jan Plefka, Plane-wave Matrix Theory from N=4 Super Yang-Mills on ℝ×S 3\mathbb{R} \times S^3, Nucl. Phys. B671:359-382, 2003 (arXiv:hep-th/0306054)

Specifically on D=11 N=1 supergravity solutions for the near horizon geometry of black M5-branes wrapped on hyperbolic 3-manifolds Σ 3=H 3/Γ\Sigma^3 = H^3/\Gamma:

  • Aristomenis Donos, Jerome Gauntlett, Nakwoo Kim, Oscar Varelam, Wrapped M5-branes, consistent truncations and AdS/CMT, JHEP 1012:003, 2010 (arXiv:1009.3805)
