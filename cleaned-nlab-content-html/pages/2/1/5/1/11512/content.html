Definition

Definition

The profinite completion of the integers is the inverse limit

ℤ^≔lim← n∈ℕ(ℤ/nℤ) \widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \coloneqq \underset{\leftarrow}{\lim}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} (\mathbb{Z}/n\mathbb{Z})

of all the cyclic groups over their canonical filtered diagram.

Proposition

This is isomorphic to the product of the p-adic integers for all prime numbers pp

ℤ^≃∏pprimeℤ p. \widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \simeq \underset{p\; prime}{\prod} \mathbb{Z}_p \,.
Remark

From this perspective the concept of the ring of adeles is natural, see there for more.

Properties

Pontryagin duality

Under Pontryagin duality, ℤ^\hat \mathbb{Z} maps to ℚ/ℤ\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}, see at Pontryagin duality for torsion abelian groups.

ℤ[p −1]/ℤ ↪ ℚ/ℤ ↪ ℝ/ℤ hom(−,ℝ/ℤ)↓ ℤ p ← ℤ^ ← ℤ \array{ &\mathbb{Z}[p^{-1}]/\mathbb{Z} &\hookrightarrow& \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z} &\hookrightarrow& \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z} \\ {}^{\mathllap{hom(-,\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z})}}\downarrow \\ &\mathbb{Z}_p &\leftarrow& \hat \mathbb{Z} &\leftarrow& \mathbb{Z} }

Extension of U(1)U(1)

There exists an extension of U(1)U(1) by the profinite integers ℤ^\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}

1→ℤ^→S ℚ 1→U(1)→1, 1 \to \widehat{\mathbb{Z}} \to S^1_{\mathbb{Q}} \to U(1) \to 1,

called the universal one–dimensional solenoid or adelic solenoid S ℚ 1S^1_{\mathbb{Q}}, which can be thought of as the limit of the pp-fold covers of U(1)=S 1U(1)=S^1.

This extension is equivalently the quotient of the adèle group of the rational numbers A ℚA_{\mathbb{Q}} by ℚ\mathbb{Q}

S ℚ 1=A ℚ/ℚ. S^1_{\mathbb{Q}} = A_{\mathbb{Q}} / \mathbb{Q}.

See (Ramakrishnan and Valenza ‘99), and (Burgos and Verjovsky ‘16) for more.
