Idea

Zuckerman induction is a special case of co-induced representations:

For 𝔤\mathfrak{g} a semisimple Lie algebra and KK a suitable algebraic? group, write (𝔤,K)Mod(\mathfrak{g}, K)Mod for the category of Harish-Chandra modules over (𝔤,K)(\mathfrak{g}, K). Then for i:T↪Ki \colon T \hookrightarrow K an algebraic subgroup there is the corresponding forgetful functor i *:(𝔤,K)Mod→(𝔤,T)Mod i^* \colon (\mathfrak{g}, K)Mod \to (\mathfrak{g}, T)Mod which restricts the representation along the inclusion.

This functor has a right adjoint coinduced representation functor

i *:(𝔤,T)Mod→(𝔤,K)Mod i_* \colon (\mathfrak{g}, T)Mod \to (\mathfrak{g}, K)Mod

and this is called the Zuckerman functor. (MP, 1).

The discussion of the derived functor of this is sometimes called cohomological induction.
