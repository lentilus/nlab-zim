Idea

A ring groupoid is a 1-truncated A ∞ A_\infty -ring.

Equivalently: A groupoid GG that is both a symmetric 2-group (G,0,⊕)(G, 0, \oplus) and a monoidal groupoid (G,1,⊗)(G, 1, \otimes) such that ⊗\otimes distributes over ⊕\oplus satisfying certain higher coherence laws (given in Kelly74).

Properties

  • The discrete groupoid of integers ℤ\mathbb{Z} is the initial ring groupoid.

  • The contractible groupoid or the trivial ring groupoid 00 is the terminal ring groupoid.
