Definition

The pillowcase orbifold is the 2-dimensional flat (complex) orbifold which is the global quotient of the 2-torus by the reflection involution z↦−zz \mapsto -z on its canonical coordinates:

Pillow≔𝕋 2⫽ reflℤ 2≔(ℝ 2/ℤ 2)⫽ reflℤ 2. Pillow \;\coloneqq \; \mathbb{T}^2 \sslash_{refl} \mathbb{Z}_2 \;\coloneqq\; (\mathbb{R}^2/\mathbb{Z}^2) \sslash_{refl} \mathbb{Z}_2 \,.

Properties

Coarse underlying space

The Weierstrass elliptic function ℘\wp, regarded as a holomorphic function with values in the Riemann sphere ℂP 1\mathbb{C}P^1, exhibits the coarse underlying topological space of the pillowcase orbifold as the 2-sphere:

℘:ℂ/ℤ[i]⟶(ℂ/ℤ[i])/ reflℤ 2⟶homeoℂP 1 \wp \;\colon\; \mathbb{C}/\mathbb{Z}[i] \longrightarrow (\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{Z}[i])/_{refl} \mathbb{Z}_2 \overset{homeo}{\longrightarrow} \mathbb{C}P^1

(e.g Mukase 2004, end of §1.4, Eskin & Okounkov 2005, p. 1, Goujard & Moeller 2018, §2.3)
