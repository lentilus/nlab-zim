Selected writings

On Bott periodicity:

  • Eldon Dyer, Richard Lashof, A topological Proof of the Bott Periodicity Theorems, Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata 54 (1961) 231–254 [doi:10.1007/BF02415354]

On Poincare duality, cobordism theory and formulating the modern form of the Pontryagin-Thom isomorphism:

  • Richard Lashof, Poincaré duality and cobordism, Trans. AMS 109 (1963), 257-277 (doi:10.1090/S0002-9947-1963-0156357-4)

On the principal bundles, spherical fibrations, Hopf construction, …:

  • Albrecht Dold, Richard Lashof, Principal quasifibrations and fibre homotopy equivalence of bundles, Illinois J. Math. Volume 3, Issue 2 (1959), 285-305 (euclid:1255455128)

On homology of iterated loop spaces:

  • Eldon Dyer, Richard Lashof, Homology of Iterated Loop Spaces, American Journal of Mathematics Vol. 84, No. 1 (Jan., 1962), pp. 35-88 (jstor:2372804)

On equivariant Tietze extension theorems:

  • Richard Lashof, The Equivariant Extension Theorem, Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society Vol. 83, No. 1 (Sep., 1981), pp. 138-140 (jstor:2043909)

On microbundles:

  • N. H. Kuiper and R. K. Lashof, Microbundles and Bundles I. Elementary Theory, Invent. Math., 1, (1966), 1 – 17.

  • N. H. Kuiper and R. K. Lashof, Microbundles and Bundles II. Semisimplicial Theory, Invent. Math., 1, (1966), 243 – 259.

On equivariant bundles (equivariant fiber bundles):

  • Richard Lashof, Melvin Rothenberg, Section 1 of: GG-Smoothing theory, p. 211-266 in: Algebraic and Geometric Topology, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics, Vol. XXXII, Part 1, American Mathematical Society 1978 (doi:10.1090/pspum/032.1, pdf)

  • Richard Lashof, Equivariant bundles, Illinois J. Math. 26(2): 257-271 (doi:10.1215/ijm/1256046796.full)

  • Richard Lashof, Peter May, Generalized equivariant bundles, Bull. Soc. Math. Belg. Sér. A 38, 265-271, 1986 (pdf, pdf)

On equivariant bundles with abelian structure group:

  • Richard Lashof, Peter May, Graeme Segal, Equivariant bundles with abelian structural group, Contemporary Mathematics, Volume 19, 1983 (doi:10.1090/conm/019, pdf, pdf)
