Selected writings

On Chern-Simons theory, D=3 gravity, p-adic AdS/CFT duality, BTZ black holes, knot theory and 3-manifolds:

  • Ingmar Saberi, Knots, Trees, and Fields: Common Ground Between Physics and Mathematics (doi:10.7907/Z9VX0DHZ, pdf)

On the p-adic AdS/CFT correspondence:

  • Matthew Heydeman, Matilde Marcolli, Ingmar Saberi, Bogdan Stoica, Tensor networks, pp-adic fields, and algebraic curves: arithmetic and the AdS 3/CFT 2AdS_3/CFT_2 correspondence (arXiv:1605.07639)

  • Ingmar Saberi, Holography and local fields, (arXiv:1801.04942)

Discussion of BTZ black holes via tensor networks in the p-adic AdS/CFT correspondence:

  • Matthew Heydeman, Matilde Marcolli, Sarthak Parikh, Ingmar Saberi, Nonarchimedean Holographic Entropy from Networks of Perfect Tensors (arXiv:1812.04057)

On spin chains in condensed matter physics:

  • Ingmar Saberi, An introduction to spin systems for mathematicians, (arXiv:1801.07270)

On holomorphic twists of superconformal algebras:

  • Ingmar Saberi, Brian Williams, Superconformal algebras and holomorphic field theories (arXiv:1910.04120)

Relating supermultiplets to L ∞ L_\infty -algebras via pure spinor-formalism and Borcherds algebra:

  • Martin Cederwall, Simon Jonsson, Jakob Palmkvist, Ingmar Saberi: Canonical supermultiplets and their Koszul duals, Commun. Math. Phys. 405 (2024) 127 [arXiv:2304.01258, doi:10.1007/s00220-024-04990-z]

On manifestly supersymmetric action functionals for 11D supergravity via pure spinor superfields on superspace and application to topologically twisted supergravity:

On super multiplets via derived geometry of pure spinor-varieties:

On D=11 topologically twisted supergravity:

On local superconformal symmetry via derived geometry:

  • Fabian Hahner, Surya Raghavendran, Ingmar Saberi, Brian R. Williams: Local superconformal algebras [arXiv:2410.08176]

On BV formalism for heterotic D=10 supergravity in relation to Courant algebroids and BCOV theory:
