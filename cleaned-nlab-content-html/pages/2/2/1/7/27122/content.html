Relation to knot invariants

This means that for ℛ\mathcal{R} any ribbon category and V∈ℛV \in \mathcal{R} any object, there is a unique ribbon functor

(1)I V ℛ:FrTang⟶ℛ I^{\mathcal{R}}_V \;\colon\; FrTang \longrightarrow \mathcal{R}

which sends the generating object 1∈FrTan1 \in FrTan to V∈ℛV \in \mathcal{R}.

Since the endomorphisms of the object 0∈FrOrTang0 \in FrOrTang are the oriented framed links this means that the functor I V ℛI^{\mathcal{R}}_V (1) restricts on endomorphisms to a collection of link invariants (with values in the endomorphism ring of VV whenever ℛ\mathcal{R} is Ab-enriched).

The standard link invariants arise this way (e.g. the HOMFLY polynomial), with the ribbon category ℛ\mathcal{R} being the category of representations (finite dimensional) of a quantum group (cf. Yetter 2001 p 118).
