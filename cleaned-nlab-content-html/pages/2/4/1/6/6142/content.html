Idea

The Gel’fand-Raikov theorem (Гельфанд-Райков) says that the irreducible unitary representations of a locally compact topological group GG separate its points.

In other words, for any two group elements g,h∈Gg, h\in G there exist an irreducible unitary representation ρ:G→U(H)\rho \colon G \to U(H) such that ρ(g)≠ρ(h)\rho(g)\neq \rho(h).
