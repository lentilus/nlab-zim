Idea

The continuous group action of a topological group GG is called “proper” if it behaves like the action of a compact topological group, without GG necessarily being compact.

In particular, the isotropy groups of a proper action are compact.

Definition

Consider

  • a topological group GG,

  • a locally compact Hausdorff space XX,

  • a group action of GG on XX (making XX a topological G-space):

    G×X ⟶ρ X (g,x) ↦ g⋅x. \array{ G \times X &\overset{\rho}{\longrightarrow}& X \\ (g,x) &\mapsto& g \cdot x \mathrlap{\,.} }

The action ρ:(g,x)↦g⋅x\rho \colon (g,x) \mapsto g \cdot x is called proper (Palais 61, Def. 1.2.2) if one of the following equivalent conditions hold (their equivalence relies on XX being locally compact and Hausdorff, Palais 61, Thm. 1.2.9, Karppinen 16, Rem. 5.2.4):

  1. (Bourbaki properness) The shear map is a proper continuous function:

    G×X ⟶proper X×X (g,x) ↦ (g⋅x,x) \array{ G \times X &\overset{proper}{\longrightarrow}& X \times X \\ (g,x) &\mapsto& (g\cdot x, x) }

    (Bourbaki 60, Ch. III, Sec. 4.4, review in Lee 00, p. 266)

  2. (Borel properness) For every compact subspace K⊂XK \subset X the subset

    (K|K)≔{g∈G|g⋅K∩K≠∅}⊂G (K \vert K) \;\coloneqq\; \big\{ g \in G \,\vert\, g \cdot K \,\cap\, K \neq \varnothing \big\} \;\subset\; G

    is compact.

    (Palais 61, Thm 1.2.9 (5), attributed there to Borel)

  3. (Palais properness) Every point x∈Xx \in X has a neighbourhood U xU_x such that every point y∈U xy \in U_x has a neighbourhood V yV_y such that

    (U x|V y)≔{g∈G|g⋅U x∩V y≠∅}⊂G (U_x \vert V_y) \;\coloneqq\; \big\{ g \in G \,\vert\, g \cdot U_x \,\cap\, V_y \neq \varnothing \big\} \;\subset\; G

    has compact closure.

    (Palais 61, Def. 1.2.2)

Examples

Lie group actions on smooth manifolds

Proposition

Let XX be a smooth manifold and let GG be a compact Lie group. Then every continuous action of GG on XX is proper.

(e.g. Lee 12, Cor. 21.6)

For more see at equivariant differential topology.
