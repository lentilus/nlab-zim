Selected writings

Formulating the string/black hole correspondence principle:

  • Gary T. Horowitz, Joseph Polchinski: A Correspondence Principle for Black Holes and Strings, Phys. Rev. D 55 (1997) 6189-6197 [arXiv:hep-th/9612146]

  • Gary T. Horowitz, Joseph Polchinski: Self Gravitating Fundamental Strings, Phys. Rev. D 55 (1997) 6189-6197 [arXiv:hep-th/9612146, doi:10.1103/PhysRevD.55.6189]

Introducing Polchinski's flow equation in renormalization:

  • Joseph Polchinski, Renormalization and effective Lagrangians , Nuclear Phys. B B231, 1984 (pdf)

Introducing orientifolds:

  • Jin Dai, R.G. Leigh, Joseph Polchinski, p. 12 of: New Connections Between String Theories, Mod. Phys. Lett. A4 (1989) 2073-2083 (spire:25758, pdf scan)

Introducing D-branes:

  • Joseph Polchinski, Dirichlet-Branes and Ramond-Ramond Charges, Phys. Rev. Lett. 75:4724-4727, 1995 (arXiv:hep-th/9510017)

    from p.7:

although it appears that we have modified the type II theory by adding something new to it, we are now arguing that these objects are actually intrinsic to any nonperturbative formulation of the type II theory; presumably one should think of them as an alternate representation of the black pp-branes

General review includes

  • Joseph Polchinski, TASI Lectures on D-Branes (arXiv:hep-th/9611050)

On orientifolds, RR-field tadpole cancellation and orientifolds of K3:

  • Eric Gimon, Joseph Polchinski, Consistency Conditions for Orientifolds and D-Manifolds, Phys. Rev. D54: 1667-1676, 1996 (arXiv:hep-th/9601038)

    (specifically for D5-D9 brane bound states dual to small instantons in heterotic string theory)

Textbook on string theory:

  • Joseph Polchinski, String theory, Monographs on Mathematical Physics, Cambridge University Press (1998)

    • vol 1: An Introduction to the Bosonic String

      doi:10.1017/CBO9780511816079

    • vol 2: Superstring Theory and Beyond

      doi:10.1017/CBO9780511618123

On Dirac charge quantization and introducing what came to be called the Polchinski completeness hypothesis:

  • Joseph Polchinski: Monopoles, Duality, and String Theory, Int. J. Mod. Phys. A 19S1 (2004) 145–156 [arXiv:hep-th/0304042, doi:10.1142/S0217751X0401866X]

On AdS/CFT duality:

  • Gary Horowitz, Joseph Polchinski, Gauge/gravity duality, gr-qc/0602037

  • Joseph Polchinski, Introduction to Gauge/Gravity Duality (arXiv:1010.6134)

On duality in string theory:

  • Joseph Polchinski: Dualities of Fields and Strings, Studies in History and Philosophy of Modern Physics 59 (C) (2017) 6-20 [arXiv:1412.5704, doi:10.1016/j.shpsb.2015.08.011]
