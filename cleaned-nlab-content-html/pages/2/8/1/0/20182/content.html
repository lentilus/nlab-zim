Idea

Given two topological spaces XX, YY one may ask for the rational homotopy type of their mapping space Maps(X,Y)Maps(X,Y). Under good conditions this is a nilpotent space of finite type and hence admits a Sullivan model from which its rationalized homotopy groups and its rational cohomology groups may be read off.

General formula

Proposition

For X,𝒜X, \mathcal{A} a pair of connected nilpotent topological spaces of rational finite type, the mapping space from XX into the rationalization L ℚ𝒜L^{\mathbb{Q}} \mathcal{A} has a (simplicial) weak homotopy equivalence

Map(X,L ℚ𝒜)≃Ω cl 1(X×Δ •;𝔩𝒜), Map\big( X, L^{\mathbb{Q}}\mathcal{A} \big) \,\simeq\, \Omega^1_{cl}\big( X \times \Delta^\bullet; \mathfrak{l}\mathcal{A} \big) \mathrlap{\,,}

where we denote by

  • 𝔩(−)\mathfrak{l}(-) the rational Whitehead L ∞ L_\infty -algebra,

  • Ω cl 1(−;𝔤)≔Hom dgcAlg(CE(𝔤),Ω PL •(−))\Omega^1_{cl}(-;\mathfrak{g}) \coloneqq Hom_{dgcAlg}\big(CE(\mathfrak{g}), \Omega^\bullet_{PL}(-)\big) the set of closed L ∞ L_\infty -algebra valued differential forms, where

    • Ω PL •(X×Δ n)\Omega^\bullet_{PL}\big(X \times \Delta^n\big) is the polynomial de Rham complex of the product space of XX with the n-simplex,

    • CE(−)CE(-) the Chevalley-Eilenberg algebra, so that CE(𝔩(−))CE\big(\mathfrak{l}(-)\big) gives the minimal Sullivan model,

and we understand the topological space on the left as its homotopy type incarnated by its singular simplicial complex.

(This is, in paraphrase, Berglund 2015 Thm. 1.4.)

Over the real numbers and in the case that XX is a smooth manifold, there is a quick re-proof of Prop. using cohesive homotopy theory:

Proposition

For

  • XX a smooth manifold,

  • 𝒜\mathcal{A} a connected nilpotent topological space of rational finite type,

there is an equivalence

Map(X,L ℝ𝒜)≃ʃΩ cl 1(X;𝔩𝒜), \mathbf{Map}\big( X, L^{\mathbb{R}} \mathcal{A} \big) \simeq \esh \mathbf{\Omega}^1_{cl}\big(X; \mathfrak{l}\mathcal{A}\big) \,,

of (geometrically discrete) smooth ∞ \infty -groupoids, where we denote by

  • Map(−,−)\mathbf{Map}(-,-) the mapping stack (internal hom),

  • L ℝ(−)L^{\mathbb{R}}(-) the ℝ \mathbb{R} -rationalization (rationalization followed by derived extension of scalars),

  • Ω cl 1(X;𝔤):U↦Hom dgcAlg(CE(𝔤),Ω dR •(U×X))\mathbf{\Omega}^1_{cl}\big(X; \mathfrak{g}\big) \,\colon\, U \mapsto Hom_{dgcAlg}\big( CE(\mathfrak{g}), \Omega^\bullet_{dR}(U \times X) \big) the moduli smooth set of closed L ∞ L_\infty -algebra valued differential forms,

  • ʃ\esh the shape modality.

Proof

This follows readily as:

ʃΩ cl 1(X;𝔩𝒜) ≃ʃMap(X,Ω cl 1(*;𝔩𝒜)) ≃Map(X,ʃΩ cl 1(*;𝔩𝒜)) =Map(X,L ℝ𝒜), \begin{aligned} \esh \mathbf{\Omega}^1_{cl}\big( X; \mathfrak{l}\mathcal{A} \big) & \simeq \esh \mathbf{Map}\Big( X, \mathbf{\Omega}^1_{cl}\big( \ast; \mathfrak{l}\mathcal{A} \big) \Big) \\ & \simeq \mathbf{Map}\Big( X, \esh \mathbf{\Omega}^1_{cl}\big( \ast; \mathfrak{l}\mathcal{A} \big) \Big) \\ & = \mathbf{Map}\Big( X, L^{\mathbb{R}} \mathcal{A} \Big) \mathrlap{\,,} \end{aligned}

where we used, in order of appearance:

  1. the formula for the plots of mapping stacks (cf. here),

  2. the smooth Oka principle to pass the shape modality into the mapping stack,

  3. the formula for shape via cohesive path ∞-groupoids to identify

    ʃΩ cl 1(*;𝔤)≃DscΩ cl 1(Δ •;𝔤), \esh \mathbf{\Omega}^1_{cl}(\ast; \mathfrak{g}) \simeq Dsc\, \Omega^1_{cl}\big(\mathbf{\Delta}^\bullet; \mathfrak{g}\big) \mathrlap{\,,}

    together with the fundamental theorem of dg-algebraic rational homotopy theory:

    L ℝ𝒜≃Ω cl 1(Δ •;𝔩𝒜), L^{\mathbb{R}}\mathcal{A} \simeq \Omega^1_{cl}\big(\mathbf{\Delta}^\bullet; \mathfrak{l}\mathcal{A}\big) \mathrlap{\,,}

    here implemented not with piecewise linear but with smooth differential forms on smooth manifolds and extended simplices Δ n\mathbf{\Delta}^n (which still satisfy the relevant extension lemma for differential forms, see there).

The global model of Prop. induces analog models for each of the connected components of the mapping space:

Proposition

Consider:

  • X,𝒜X, \mathcal{A} a pair of connected nilpotent CW-complexes of rational finite type,

    with XX a finite CW complex or 𝒜\mathcal{A} having a finite Postnikov tower,

  • ϕ:X→𝒜\phi \colon X \to \mathcal{A} a continuous map.

Then a dg-Lie algebra model for the rational homotopy type of the mapping space Map(X;𝒜)Map\big(X; \mathcal{A}\big) in the connected component of ϕ\phi is given by the twisted tensor product

(Ω PL •(X)⊗ ℚ𝔩𝒜) ξ ϕ⟨0⟩ \big( \Omega^\bullet_{PL}(X) \textstyle{ \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} } \mathfrak{l} \mathcal{A} \big)^{\xi_\phi} \langle 0 \rangle

where :

  • Ω PL •(−)\Omega^\bullet_{PL}(-) denotes the PL de Rham dgc-algebra,

  • 𝔩(−)\mathfrak{l}(-) denotes the dg-Lie algebra model.

  • ξ ϕ∈MC(Ω PL •(X)⊗𝔩𝒜)\xi_\phi \in MC\big( \Omega^\bullet_{PL}(X) \otimes \mathfrak{l}\mathcal{A}\big) is the Maurer-Cartan element corresponding to the pullback of differential forms

    ϕ *:CE(𝔩𝒜)⟶Ω PL •(X), \phi^\ast \,\colon\, CE(\mathfrak{l}\mathcal{A}) \longrightarrow \Omega^\bullet_{PL}(X) \mathrlap{\,,}

  • (−) ξ ϕ(-)^{\xi_\phi} means that the differential of the tensor dg-Lie algebra is twisted as

    d 𝔩ϕ(−)≔d(−)+[ξ ϕ,−], d^{\mathfrak{l}\phi}(-) \coloneqq \mathrm{d}(-) + \big[\xi_\phi, -\big] \mathrlap{\,,}

  • (−)⟨−⟩(-)\langle-\rangle denotes the connective cover (discarding negative degrees and restricting to cycles in degree=0).

(Lazarev 2013 Thm. 8.1, cf. Berglund 2015 Thm. 1.5, Buijs, Felix & Murillo 2012 Thm. 3.1)

Rational Cohomotopy spaces

We discuss results on the rational homotopy type of spaces of maps into an n-sphere, hence rational Cohomotopy cocycle spaces.

Proposition

(rational homotopy type of space of maps from n-sphere to itself)

Let n∈ℕn \in \mathbb{N} be a natural number and f:S n→S nf\colon S^n \to S^n a continuous function from the n-sphere to itself. Then the connected component Maps f(S n,S n)Maps_f\big( S^n, S^n\big) of the space of maps which contains this map has the following rational homotopy type:

(1)Maps f(S n,S n)≃ ℚ{S n×S n−1 | neven,deg(f)=0 S 2n−1 | neven,deg(f)≠0 S n | nodd Maps_f\big( S^n, S^n\big) \;\simeq_{\mathbb{Q}}\; \left\{ \array{ S^n \times S^{n-1} &\vert& n\,\text{even}\,, deg(f) = 0 \\ S^{2n-1} &\vert& n \, \text{even}\,, deg(f) \neq 0 \\ S^n &\vert& n\, \text{odd} } \right.

where deg(f)deg(f) is the degree of ff.

Moreover, under the canonical morphism expressing the canonical action of the special orthogonal group SO(n+1)SO(n+1) on S n=S(ℝ n+1)S^n = S\big( \mathbb{R}^{n+1}\big) (regarded as the unit sphere in (n+1)(n+1)-dimensional Cartesian space) we have that on ordinary homology

H •(SO(n+1)) ⟶ H •(Maps f=id(S n,S n)) \array{ H_\bullet\Big( SO\big( n+ 1 \big) \Big) &\longrightarrow& H_\bullet\Big( Maps_{f = id}\big( S^n, S^n \big) \Big) }

the generator in {H 2n+1(SO(n+1),ℚ)≃ℚ | neven H n(SO(n+1),ℚ)≃ℚ | nodd\left\{ \array{ H_{2n+1}\big( SO(n+1), \mathbb{Q} \big) \simeq \mathbb{Q} &\vert& n\, \text{even} \\ H_{n}\big( SO(n+1), \mathbb{Q} \big) \simeq \mathbb{Q} &\vert& n\, \text{odd} } \right. maps to the fundamental class of the respective spheres in (1), all other generators mapping to zero.

(Møller-Raussen 85, Example 2.5, Cohen-Voronov 05, Lemma 5.3.5)

Proposition

(rational cohomology of iterated loop space of the 2k-sphere)

Let

1≤D<n=2k∈ℕ 1 \leq D \lt n = 2k \in \mathbb{N}

(hence two positive natural numbers, one of them required to be even and the other required to be smaller than the first) and consider the D-fold loop space Ω DS n\Omega^D S^n of the n-sphere.

Its rational cohomology ring is the free graded-commutative algebra over ℚ\mathbb{Q} on one generator e n−De_{n-D} of degree n−Dn - D and one generator a 2n−D−1a_{2n - D - 1} of degree 2n−D−12n - D - 1:

H •(Ω DS n,ℚ)≃ℚ[ω n−D,ω 2n−1−D]. H^\bullet \big( \Omega^D S^n , \mathbb{Q} \big) \;\simeq\; \mathbb{Q}\big[ \omega_{n - D}, \omega_{2n - 1 - D} \big] \,.

(by this Prop. at Sullivan model of based loop space; see also Kallel-Sjerve 99, Prop. 4.10)

For the edge case Ω DS D\Omega^D S^D the above formula does not apply, since Ω D−1S D\Omega^{D-1} S^D is not simply connected (its fundamental group is π 1(Ω D−1S D)=π 0(Ω DS D)=π D(S D)=ℤ\pi_1\big( \Omega^{D-1}S^D \big) = \pi_0 \big(\Omega^D S^D\big) = \pi_D(S^D) = \mathbb{Z}, the 0th stable homotopy group of spheres).
