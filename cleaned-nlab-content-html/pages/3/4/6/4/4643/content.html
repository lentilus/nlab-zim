Idea

The quantum geometric Langlands correspondence is a conjectured equivalence between the derived category of certain twisted D-modules on the moduli stack of GG-principal bundles over some complex curve for some reductive group GG, and that of DD-modules with a dual twist on the stack of LG{}^L G-bundles, for LG{}^L G the Langlands dual group.

In a certain limit of the twisting parameter the DD-modules on one side of this correspondence become equivalent to just 𝒪\mathcal{O}-modules, but on the moduli space of local systems. This limit of the quantum correspondence reproduces the statement of the (ordinary) geometric Langlands correspondence.

Slightly more precisely, writing Bun G ∘Bun_G^\circ for the connected component of the moduli stack of GG-bundles, there is a line bundle denoted ℒ ⊗k\mathcal{L}^{\otimes k} on Bun G ∘Bun_G^\circ and dually a line bundle ℒ^ ⊗k\hat \mathcal{L}^{\otimes k} on Bun LG ∘Bun_{{}^L G}^\circ and these serve to defined sheaves 𝒟 (k,λ)\mathcal{D}^{(k,\lambda)} of twisted differential operators on Bun G ∘Bun_{G}^\circ and dually sheaves 𝒢^ (k,λ)\hat \mathcal{G}^{(k,\lambda)} of twisted differential operators on Bun LG ∘Bun_{{}^L G}^\circ, where the parameters lie in

(k,λ)∈ℂℙ 1×ℂ. (k, \lambda) \in \mathbb{CP}^1 \times \mathbb{C} \,.

Writing then 𝒟 k,λMod(Bun G)\mathcal{D}^{k,\lambda} Mod(Bun_{G}) and 𝒟^ k,λMod(Bun LG)\hat \mathcal{D}^{k,\lambda} Mod(Bun_{{}^L G}), respectively, for the derived categories of modules over these sheaves, the conjectured statement is:

Quantum geometric Langlands correspondence

There is an equivalence

𝒟 k,λMod(Bun G)≃𝒟^ 1k,λMod(Bun LG). \mathcal{D}^{k,\lambda} Mod(Bun_{G}) \simeq \hat \mathcal{D}^{\frac{1}{k},\lambda} Mod(Bun_{{}^L G}) \,.

(In fact kk and 1k\frac{1}{k} here should further be shifted by the dual Coxeter number of GG and LG{}^L G, respectively.)

Embedding in string theory

Where the plain geometric Langlands correspondence is meant to be a shadow of the 6d (2,0)-superconformal field theory, the quantum version is meant to correspondingly relate to 6d little string theory (Aganagic-Frenkel-Okounkov 17) (which turns into the 6d QFT in the limit that the strings shrink to points).

Examples

Limiting cases

The twisted sheaves of differential operators 𝒟 k,λ\mathcal{D}^{k,\lambda} have the following limits:

  • For λ≠0\lambda \neq 0 and k≠∞k \neq \infty this is the sheaf of differential operators acting on ℒ ⊗k\mathcal{L}^{\otimes k};

  • for λ≠0\lambda \neq 0 and k=∞k = \infty this is the pushforward p *(𝒪 Loc G)p_*(\mathcal{O}_{Loc_G}) of the sheaf of 𝒪\mathcal{O}-modules along the canonical P:Loc G→Bun GP : Loc_G \to Bun_G that sends a local system to its underlying bundle;

  • for λ=0\lambda = 0 and kk arbitrary this is p *(𝒪 T *Bun G ∘)p_*(\mathcal{O}_{T^* Bun_G^\circ}).

References

The statement of the quantum geometric Langlands correspondence is surveyed on page 70-71 of

  • Edward Frenkel, Lectures on the Langlands Program and Conformal Field Theory (arXiv:hep-th/0512172).

with more details in:

  • Dennis Gaitsgory, Quantum Langlands Correspondence [arXiv:1601.05279]

The construction of the correspondence in the abelian case, where it is given by a Fourier-Mukai transform, is given in

  • A. Polishchuk and M. Rothstein, Fourier transform for D-algebras , DukeMath. J. 109 (2001) 123–146.

An interpretation of the quantum Langlands correspondence in terms of the B-model topological string is given in

  • Anton Kapustin, A Note on Quantum Geometric Langlands Duality, Gauge Theory, and Quantization of the Moduli Space of Flat Connections (arXiv:0811.3264)

A general quantum geometric Langlands correspondence is produced in
