Definition

Definition

A G-∞-category 𝒞\mathcal{C} is G-stable if

  1. 𝒞\mathcal{C} is G-semiadditive, and

  2. For all H⊂GH \subset G, 𝒞 H\mathcal{C}_H is a stable ∞-category.
