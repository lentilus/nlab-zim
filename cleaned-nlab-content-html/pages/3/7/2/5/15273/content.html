Idea

Where ordinary 3d Chern-Simons theory is given by an action functional with values in the circle group ℝ/ℤ\mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z} on a space of special unitary group-principal connections, its “analytic continuation”(Gukov 03, Witten 10) instead is defined on complex special linear group-principal connections and its values are elements in ℂ/ℤ\mathbb{C}/\mathbb{Z} (see also at Chern-Simons theory with complex gauge group).

The Wilson line quantum observables of analytically continued Chern-Simons theory are accordingly analytic continuations of knot invariants (Garoufalidis 07).

Properties
