Idea

U(2)U(2) is the unitary group in dimension 2. It is used to describe almost complex structures on 4-manifolds.

Properties

Proposition

One has exceptional isomorphisms between U(2)U(2) and the spinᶜ group in dimension 3 as well as the spinʰ group in dimension 2:

U(2)≅Spin c(3)≅Spin h(2). U(2) \cong Spin^\mathrm{c}(3) \cong Spin^\mathrm{h}(2).

(Gompf & Stipsicz 99, Ex. 2.4.14, Nicolaescu 00, Ex. 1.3.9)

Proof

Using the exceptional isomorphism Spin(3)≅SU(2)Spin(3)\cong SU(2) yields:

Spin c(3)≅(Spin(3)×U(1))/ℤ 2=(SU(2)×U(1))/ℤ 2≅U(2) Spin^\mathrm{c}(3) \cong(Spin(3)\times U(1))/\mathbb{Z}_2 =(SU(2)\times U(1))/\mathbb{Z}_2 \cong U(2)

Using the exceptional isomorphism Spin(2)≅U(1)Spin(2)\cong U(1) as well as Sp(1)≅SU(2)Sp(1)\cong SU(2) yields:

Spin h(2)≅(Spin(2)×Sp(1))/ℤ 2=(SU(2)×U(1))/ℤ 2≅U(2) Spin^\mathrm{h}(2) \cong(Spin(2)\times Sp(1))/\mathbb{Z}_2 =(SU(2)\times U(1))/\mathbb{Z}_2 \cong U(2)
