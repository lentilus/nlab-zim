Ivan T. Todorov

NB: There is also Ivan G. Todorov.

Ivan T. Todorov (1933-2025) was a Bulgarian theoretical and mathematical physicist, a specialist in quantum field theory, especially conformal field theory as well as representation theoretic methods in quantum field theory.

Selected writings

On quantum field theory:

  • Nikolay N. Bogoliubov, A. A. Logunov, Ivan T. Todorov, Introduction to axiomatic quantum field theory (1975) [ark:13960/s29mp6cvfk8, inSpire:103893]

Introduction of coherent states for the quantum group SU q(3)SU_q(3):

  • H. Sazdjian, Y. S. Stanev, I. T. Todorov, SU(3)SU(3)-coherent state operators and invariant correlation functions and their quantum group counterparts, J. Math. Phys. 36 (1995) 2030–2052.

  • L. K. Hadjiivanov, R. R. Paunov, I. T. Todorov, U qU_q-covariant oscillators and vertex operators, J. Math. Phys. 33, 1379 (1992) doi

On braid group representations via monodromy of the Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov connection on conformal blocks:

  • Ivan Todorov, Ludmil Hadjiivanov, Monodromy Representations of the Braid Group, Phys. Atom. Nucl. 64 (2001) 2059-2068; Yad.Fiz. 64 (2001) 2149-2158 [arXiv:hep-th/0012099, doi:10.1134/1.1432899]
