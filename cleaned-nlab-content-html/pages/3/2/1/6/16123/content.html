Idea

A polynomial all of whose monomial terms have the same degree. See also polynomial.

Definition

If RR is a commutative rig, then the set R n[X 1,...,X q]R_{n}[X_{1},...,X_{q}] of homogeneous polynomials of degree nn with variables X 1,...,X qX_{1},...,X_{q} is given by all the expressions of the form ∑0≤i 1,...,i q≤n i 1+...+i q=na i 1,...,i qX 1 i 1...X q i q\underset{\substack{0\le i_{1},...,i_{q} \le n\\ i_{1}+...+i_{q} = n}}{\sum} a_{i_{1},...,i_{q}}X_{1}^{i_{1}}...X_{q}^{i_{q}}.
