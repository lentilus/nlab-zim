Idea

The special unitary group in complex dimension 5.

Embedding of the gauge group of the standard model

SU(5)SU(5) is of particular importence for the gauge group of the standard model. It is composed of the:

  • first unitary group U(1)U(1) for the electromagnetic interaction described by quantum electrodynamics (QED). dimU(1)=1dim U(1)=1 corresponds to the one photon.
  • second special unitary group SU(2)SU(2) for the weak interaction described by quantum flavordynamics (QFD). dimSU(2)=3dim SU(2)=3 corresponds to the three massive gauge bosons W −W^-, W +W^+ and ZZ.
  • third special unitary group SU(3)SU(3) for the strong interaction described by quantum chromodynamics (QCD). dimSU(3)=8dim SU(3)=8 corresponds to the eight types of gluons.

It is not the product U(1)×SU(2)×SU(3)U(1)\times SU(2)\times SU(3), but instead its quotient group G SM=(U(1)×SU(2)×SU(3))/ℤ 6G_SM=(U(1)\times SU(2)\times SU(3))/\mathbb{Z}_6 (with all known particles invariant under this ℤ 6\mathbb{Z}_6 action). There is an embedding:

Φ:U(1)×SU(2)×SU(3)↪SU(5),(e iφ,U,V)↦(e −3iφU 0 0 e 2iφV) \Phi\colon U(1)\times SU(2)\times SU(3)\hookrightarrow SU(5), (e^{i\varphi},U,V)\mapsto\begin{pmatrix} e^{-3i\varphi}U & 0 \\ 0 & e^{2i\varphi}V \end{pmatrix}

It has ker(Φ)≅ℤ 6ker(\Phi)\cong\mathbb{Z}_6 generated by (ζ 6,ζ 31 2,ζ 21 3)(\zeta_6,\zeta_3\mathbf{1}_2,\zeta_2\mathbf{1}_3) with the primitive roots of unity ζ 6,ζ 2=ζ 6 3,ζ 3=ζ 6 2∈U(1)\zeta_6,\zeta_2=\zeta_6^3,\zeta_3=\zeta_6^2\in U(1). Hence G SM≅img(Φ)≅S(U(2)×U(3))G_SM\cong img(\Phi)\cong S(U(2)\times U(3)).
