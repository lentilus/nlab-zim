Idea

Jordan algebras were invented to axiomatize some properties of the Jordan product a∘b=(ab+ba)/2a \circ b = (a b + b a)/2 of self-adjoint complex matrices, which serve as observables in quantum mechanics. As work proceeded, some researchers found it convenient to focus on another binary operation on self-adjoint matrices,

U a(b)=2a∘(a∘b)−(a∘a)∘b=aba. U_a (b) = 2 a \circ (a \circ b) - (a \circ a) \circ b = a b a \, .

Axiomatizing the properties of this operation led to the definition of a quadratic Jordan algebra. Later work led to the definitions of Jordan triple system and Jordan pair.
