Idea

A categorification of the notion of σ \sigma -frame.

Definition

A σ\sigma-pretopos is a pretopos ℰ\mathcal{E} where all countable unions of subobjects (exist and) are pullback-stable.

Girard-esque axioms

A σ\sigma-pretopos is

  • a category

  • with a generating set,

  • with all finite limits,

  • with all countable coproducts, which are disjoint, and pullback-stable,

  • where all congruences have effective quotient objects, which are also pullback-stable.

Examples

  • Any infinitary pretopos is a σ\sigma-topos.

  • The category of countable sets (for instance, subquotients of the natural numbers ℕ\mathbb{N}) is a σ\sigma-pretopos.

  • The category of sets in the predicative set theory CZF ExpCZF_{Exp} from (Simpson-Streicher 2012) is a σ\sigma-pretopos.
