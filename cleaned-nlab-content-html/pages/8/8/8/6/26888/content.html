Capelli identity

Idea

The classical Capelli identity is an equality of differential operators written in a form roughly resembling the classical identity det(AB)=det(A)det(B)det(A B) = det(A) det(B) where however at the right hand side are true determinants of commutative entries and at the left side is a column determinant whose entries are (corrections of) non-commuting vector fields, so called polarization operators.

There are numerous generalizations.
