Selected writings

On cosmic inflation in supergravity:

  • Dimitri V. Nanopoulos, Keith A. Olive, Mark Srednicki, K. Tamvakis: Primordial inflation in simple supergravity, Phys. Lett. B 123 (1983) 41–44 [doi:10.1016/0370-2693(83)90954-1]

The original suggestion that the lightest supersymmetric particle (LSP) would be a natural dark matter candidate:

  • Ching-Ming Chen, Tianjun Li, Van Eric Mayes, Dimitri Nanopoulos, A Realistic World from Intersecting D6-Branes, Phys.Lett.B665:267-270, 2008 (arXiv:hep-th/0703280, doi:10.1016/j.physletb.2008.06.024)
