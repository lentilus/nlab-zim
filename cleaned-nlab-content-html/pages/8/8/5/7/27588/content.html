Idea

Roughly, the Habiro ring is a ring of functions defined on formal neighborhoods of all roots of unity simultaneously, satisfying appropriate gluing conditions.

Definition

The Habiro ring over ℤ\mathbb{Z} is defined as an inverse limit:

ℋ ℤ≃lim nℤ[q]/((q;q) n), \mathcal{H}_{\mathbb{Z}} \;\simeq\; \lim_n \, \mathbb{Z}[q]/\big( (q;q)_n \big) \,,

where (q;q) n=∏ i=1 n(1−q i)(q;q)_n = \prod_{i=1}^n(1-q^i), or equivalently, an inverse limit:

ℋ ℤ≃lim n,mℤ[q]/((1−q n) m), \mathcal{H}_{\mathbb{Z}} \;\simeq\; \lim_{n,m} \, \mathbb{Z}[q]/\big((1-q^n)^m\big) \,,

Properties

For any root of unity ω\omega and f∈ℋ ℤf \in \mathcal{H}_{\mathbb{Z}}, one defines f ω(x)≔f(ω+x)∈ℤ[ω][[x]]f_\omega(x) \coloneqq f(\omega + x) \in \mathbb{Z}[\omega][[x]]. These satisfy compatibility conditions for varying ω\omega.
