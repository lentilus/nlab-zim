Selected writings

On model structures on chain complexes in homological algebra:

On Cayley-Dickson algebras:

  • Daniel K. Biss, J. Daniel Christensen, Daniel Dugger, Daniel Isaksen, Eigentheory of Cayley-Dickson algebras, Forum Mathematicum 21(5) (2009), 833-851 (arxiv:0905.2987)

On the D-topology on diffeological spaces:

  • J. Daniel Christensen, Gord Sinnamon, Enxin Wu, The DD-topology for diffeological spaces, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 272 (1), 87-110, 2014 (arXiv:1302.2935, doi:10.2140/pjm.2014.272.87)

On the homotopy theory of diffeological spaces via their cohesive shape:

  • J. Daniel Christensen, Enxin Wu: The homotopy theory of diffeological spaces, New York J. Math. 20 (2014) 1269-1303 [arXiv:1311.6394, nyjm:2014/20-59]

On localization (in algebraic topology) formulated in modal homotopy type theory:

On modal homotopy type theory:

  • J. Daniel Christensen, Egbert Rijke, Characterizations of modalities and lex modalities (arXiv:2008.03538)

On delooping, H-spaces and Eilenberg-MacLane spaces in homotopy type theory:
