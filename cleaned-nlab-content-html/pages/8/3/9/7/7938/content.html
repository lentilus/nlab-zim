Idea

The concept of coherent state in quantum mechanics/quantum physics may be formalized in the context of geometric quantization, The original definition is due to Rawnsley 1977, further developments include Kirwin 2007.

Related concepts

  • quantization of Chern-Simons theory

References

The original reference that interpreted coherent states in geometric quantization over Kähler manifolds:

  • John H. Rawnsley: Coherent states and Kähler manifolds, Quart. J. Math. Oxford 2 28 (1977) 403-415 [doi:10.1093/qmath/28.4.403]

Review and further developments:

  • Mauro Spera, On Kählerian coherent states (djvu)
