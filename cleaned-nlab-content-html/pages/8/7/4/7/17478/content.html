Idea

The quaternion unitary group, often denoted Sp(n)Sp(n) in the math literature (rarely but alternatively: U(n,ℍ)\mathrm{U}(n, \mathbb{H}), is a Lie group which is the analog of the unitary group as one passes from the complex numbers to the quaternions, hence it is the group of quaternion-unitary transformations of the quaternionic vector space ℍ n\mathbb{H}^n.

Beware that this group is also called the compact symplectic group, since both it and the symplectic group Sp(2n,ℝ)Sp(2n, \mathbb{R}) are real forms of the complex Lie group Sp(2n,ℂ)Sp(2n,\mathbb{C}), but Sp(n)Sp(n) is the compact form.

Definition

Definition

(1)Sp(n)≡U(n,ℍ)≔{U∈Mat n×n(ℍ)|U⋅U †=I n} Sp(n) \equiv U(n,\mathbb{H}) \;\coloneqq\; \Big\{ U \in Mat_{n \times n}(\mathbb{H}) \;\Big\vert\; U \cdot U^\dagger = I_n \Big\}

(cf. Zhang 1997, p. 28)

Remark

The condition in (1) is indeed sufficient, since for B∈Mat n×n(ℍ)B \in Mat_{n \times n}(\mathbb{H}) we have

B⋅B †=I n ⇔ B †⋅B=I n \begin{array}{cl} & B \cdot B^\dagger = I_n \\ \Leftrightarrow & B^\dagger \cdot B = I_n \end{array}

(cf. Zhang 1997, Prop. 4.1).
