Idea

Superspheres are supergeometric analogs of spheres.

Coset representations

  • The supersphere S 2|2S^{2|2} is the super coset space UOSp(1|2)/U(1)UOSp(1|2)/U(1).

  • The supersphere S r−1|2sS^{r-1|2s} is the super coset space OSp(r|2s)/OSp(r−1|2s)OSp(r|2s)/OSp(r-1|2s) of orthosymplectic super Lie groups (GJS 18).
