Definition

Given a (topological) ground field 𝕂\mathbb{K} (or more generally a topological ground ring), its projective spaces 𝕂P n\mathbb{K}P^n canonical form a sequence of subspaces

(1)𝕂P n ↪−− 𝕂P n+1 [x 1:⋯:x n+1] ↦ [x 1:⋯:x n+1:0]. \begin{array}{ccc} \mathbb{K}P^n &\xhookrightarrow{\phantom{--}}& \mathbb{K}P^{n+1} \\ [x_1 \colon \cdots \colon x_{n+1}] &\mapsto& [x_1 \colon \cdots \colon x_{n+1} \colon 0] \mathrlap{\,.} \end{array}

Definition

The infinite projective space 𝕂P ∞\mathbb{K}P^\infty over 𝕂\mathbb{K} is the union of the 𝕂P n\mathbb{K}P^n, hence the colimit over the above sequence (1):

𝕂P ∞≔lim⟶n→∞𝕂P n. \mathbb{K}P^\infty \;\coloneqq\; \underset{\underset{n \to \infty}{\longrightarrow}}{\lim} \, \mathbb{K}P^n \,.

Examples

Example

The infinite projective space over the real numbers, 𝕂≔ℝ\mathbb{K} \coloneqq \mathbb{R}, is a model for (the homotopy type of) the classifying space of the cyclic group of order 2:

ℝP ∞≃BC 2. \mathbb{R}P^\infty \;\simeq\; B C_2 \,.

Example

The infinite projective space over the complex numbers, 𝕂≔ℂ\mathbb{K} \coloneqq \mathbb{C}, is a model for (the homotopy type of) the classifying space of the circle group U(1):

ℂP ∞≃BU(1). \mathbb{C}P^\infty \;\simeq\; B \mathrm{U}(1) \,.

Example

The infinite projective space over the quaternions, 𝕂≔ℍ\mathbb{K} \coloneqq \mathbb{H}, is a model for (the homotopy type of) the classifying space of the quaternion unitary group Sp(1):

ℍP ∞≃BSp(1). \mathbb{H}P^\infty \;\simeq\; B \mathrm{Sp}(1) \,.
