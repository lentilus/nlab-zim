Statement

Proposition

Robinson’s Joint Consistency Theorem: If T 1T_1 is a consistent L 1L_1-theory, and T 2T_2 is a consistent L 2L_2-theory, then their union T 1∪T 2T_1 \cup T_2 is consistent when their intersection T 1∩T 2T_1 \cap T_2 is complete as an (L 1∩L 2)(L_1 \cap L_2)-theory.

Proof

Apply the Craig interpolation theorem.

This may be used to demonstrate the Beth definability theorem.
