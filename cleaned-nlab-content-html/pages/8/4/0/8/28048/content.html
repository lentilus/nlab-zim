Definition

Denote by

  • ℋ\mathscr{H} the countably-infinite-dimensional separable Hilbert space,

  • ℬ(ℋ)\mathcal{B}(\mathscr{H}) the norm topology-space of bounded operators,

  • 𝒦(ℋ)⊂ℬ(ℋ)\mathcal{K}(\mathscr{H}) \subset \mathcal{B}(\mathscr{H}) the subset of compact operators,

  • GL(ℋ)⊂ℬ(ℋ)GL(\mathscr{H}) \subset \mathcal{B}(\mathscr{H}) the general linear group,

  • U(ℋ)⊂GL(ℋ)\mathrm{U}(\mathscr{H}) \subset GL(\mathscr{H}) the unitary group (cf. at U(ℋ)).

Definition

The Fredholm group of ℋ\mathscr{H} is the subgroup of the general linear group of those elements which differ from the identity operator by a compact operator:

GL c(ℋ)≔{ϕ∈GL(ℋ)|ϕ−id∈𝒦(ℋ)}. GL^c(\mathscr{H}) \coloneqq \big\{ \phi \in GL(\mathscr{H}) \,\big\vert\, \phi - id \in \mathcal{K}(\mathscr{H}) \big\} \,.

(cf. Atiyah & Singer 1969 pp 10, Swanson 1978 p 191, Mukherjea 1970 p. 653)

Analogously, the unitary Fredholm group is the subgroup of U(ℋ) of those elements which differ from the identity operator by a compact operator:

U c(ℋ)≔{U∈U(ℋ)|U−id∈𝒦(ℋ)}. U^c(\mathscr{H}) \coloneqq \big\{ U \in \mathrm{U}(\mathscr{H}) \,\big\vert\, U - id \in \mathcal{K}(\mathscr{H}) \big\} \,.

(cf. Atiyah & Singer 1969 pp 10, Andruchow & Larotonda 2010, DSBW 2023 p. 87, 247)
