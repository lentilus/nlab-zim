Idea

Einstein-Dirac theory is a theory (physics) or model in physics, which combines general relativity (“Einstein theory”) with the Dirac equation (“Dirac theory”). It describes gravity coupled to fermionic matter. (Additionally coupling gravity to its spin is described by Einstein-Cartan-Dirac theory.)
