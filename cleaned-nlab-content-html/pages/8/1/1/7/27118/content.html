Birkhoff's subdirect representation theorem

This is about one of Birkhoff’s theorems in universal algebra (1944), an earlier one (from 1936) is the Birkhoff's variety theorem (HSP theorem). Beware also of other well known Birkhoff's theorems.

Literature

The original reference is

  • Garrett Birkhoff, Subdirect unions in universal algebra, Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 50 (1944) 764–768 pdf
