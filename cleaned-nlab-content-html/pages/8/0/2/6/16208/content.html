Idea

Lean is a proof assistant/proof verifier based on dependent type theory.

Like Rocq and Agda, it may be used to implement homotopy type theory. Unlike (these) other proof assistants, Lean has become widely adopted in the mathematics community.

Formalized mathematics

Some mathematics that has been formalized in Lean (in particular in the Xena project):

  • basic synthetic homotopy theory (HoTT)

    • github.com/rzrn/ground_zero

    • github.com/gebner/hott3

  • homotopy groups:

    • github.com/Vilin97/homotopy-groups-lean

  • spectral sequences

    • github.com/cmu-phil/Spectral

  • perfectoid spaces

    • github.com/leanprover-community/lean-perfectoid-spaces

  • schemes

    • The Stacks Project

  • The main theorem of liquid vector spaces

    • github.com/leanprover-community/lean-liquid

  • physics

    • PhysLean, arXiv:2405.08863

Aside from these specialised projects, a large amount of mathematics has also been formalised in Mathlib, the central library for mathematics in Lean; an overview over some of the contents of the library can be found here, though it is far from comprehensive.
